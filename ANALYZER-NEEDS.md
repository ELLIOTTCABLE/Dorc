Analyzer/engine data-needs
==========================

The living registry of *what the Dorc analysis-engine must represent, compute, track, or expose* — and
*which part of Dorc needs it*. This is `ananeed-1` (consumers) × `ananeed-2` (information), the input to
`ananeed-3` (which algorithms/data-structures satisfy it — see `Research/plans/170`). LLM-generated,
add a row when a feature implies a new datum. Elaboration, prior-art, and the algorithm-matching live in
`plans/170` (do not put prose here).

Seeded round-17 by a 7-way neutral fan-out + a 3-way adversarial gap-hunt over the planning corpus
(rounds 00–16, all worktrees); raw extraction + method in `Research/notes/172`. Rows are deliberately
over-inclusive. **Refreshed 2026-07-05 (round-24 close audit):** statuses re-graded against the
ternary-verdict / claim-tier / footprint world (rounds 23–24; settled law in `notes/23O` §2 +
`spike/CLAUDE.md`); new section **O** carries the verdict/license/frame machinery those rounds minted;
superseded rows are tombstoned in place (`X`), never silently dropped. **Refreshed 2026-07-17
(block-settle + block-rebuild stages 1–4b):** re-graded against the settled entity-algebra /
authored-surface / context-entry world (rulings `plans/271`; specs `notes/272`–`277` + `plans/27C`;
landings ledger `notes/27D`); new section **P** carries the round-27 machinery; the stage-1 respell's
renames (`touches()`→`disturbs()`, `reaches()`→`disturbance_reaches_only()`, `is_diverged` ditched,
`.prop`→`#`→`@` selector) applied to rows in place. **Refreshed
2026-07-18:** §F gains the user-aid machinery rows (collapse-evidence ·
authored-decline-class · whylog-durable; build phase `notes/27V`/`27W`); the aid-side
sibling registry is root `AID-NEEDS.md`.

**Columns** — `need` (slug, `an-*`) · `information` (the datum) · `needed-by` (consumer/phase/feature) ·
`dual` (paired half, if any — many needs come in tension-pairs) · `refs` (pointers into `Research/` /
root docs; not exhaustive) · `st` (status).
**`st`** — `B` built in a spike (round-16 or the round-20+ take-3 line) · `S` specified in design, not built
· `D` designed-but-deferred · `O` open decision (high-lock) · `W` welded (settled) · `X` retired/superseded
(tombstone; reason in-row).
Inside cells, `/` replaces `|`; `↔` marks a dual.
