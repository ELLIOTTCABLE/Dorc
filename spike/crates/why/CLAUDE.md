# `dorc-why`

The receipt-backed why RECONSTRUCTION plane (`30V` §3/§5): recorded facts joined onto one
typed model, a pure function of injected inputs. Report-plane only — nothing here may ever
feed a decision (`two-plane-aid-law`). Read `spike/CLAUDE.md` first; registry discipline:
one rule per bullet, slugged, append.

- **inv-why-deps-stay-report-side** — deps are `dorc-receipt` + `dorc-aid` ONLY: never
  `dorc-core`, never a kernel crate, never `weft` (rendering is the cli edge's job), and
  never name `Reingested` — facts arrive through `receipt::report`'s sealed surface, and
  arbitrary values exit only through its encoder interface.
- **inv-known-held-never-convert** — `Known<T>` (is this answerable/wired at all) and
  `Held<T>` (what came back, failed-but-tried states included) stay separate by
  construction: no conversion in either direction compiles; `KnowableNYI` is
  argument-free and census-gated; `Unknowable`'s reason is compile-time-only, consumed
  and dropped at the seat. Laundering NYI into Unknowable is the named failure-mode this
  shape exists to prevent.
- **absence-carries-its-cause** — every absence names WHOSE it is (carrier-lacks vs
  report-api-lacks vs not-relevant vs run-held-no-value …). An audit that cannot separate
  a carrier hole from a read-surface hole sends readers to widen a durable that needs no
  widening. `Reconstruction::audit()` is DERIVED from the population, never assembled
  beside it.
- **a-voice-set-is-its-own-leaf** — a speech ACT and the VOICES performing it are
  separately knowable: a document can record the act and name nobody. Folding them makes
  an unnamed speaker read as an unknown act.
- **inv-datum-fields-mint-at-leaves** — `Datum`'s five fields are private behind one
  all-five-by-value mint; no container-level carriage of any of the five kinds exists in
  this crate (`30V` §3: hoisting creates laundering/drop pressure).
- **inv-why-is-dst-clean** — pure data: no clock, RNG, filesystem, or network, directly
  or transitively; deterministic iteration everywhere order is observable; a dependency
  added here must prove it carries no nondeterminism.
