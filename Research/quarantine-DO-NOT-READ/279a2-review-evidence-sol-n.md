## 279a2-package-holds — claims that survived the first full package read

- The ternary coordinate relation captures a genuine safety inversion: `same` may
  license transport, `provably-disjoint` may license survival only behind the flag,
  and `unknown` is the sole safe answer for both consumers.
- `cmd__lend_map()` and `invariant:<axis>` repair earlier negative-space licensing:
  every dimension is enumerated and cross-context invariance requires a positive,
  pointable speech act. Missing information walls rather than identifying.
- Keying is kept license-free and barred from survival. The address-derived topology
  work explicitly refuses the seductive but unsound address-inequality ⇒ referent-
  inequality step.
- Wrapper and eval'er designs keep ordinary books configuration-free and preserve the
  tool-oracle / wrapper-oracle no-awareness referendum. Their unresolved transform
  law is flagged and kept out of the rebuild gate rather than silently assumed.
- The value work identifies both the captured question and its answer as independently
  stale, and reserves the second value-pass/fold-time route plus literal-provenance
  representation before the rebuild bakes those seams.
- Host interaction for records, captures, and shim setup is kept at orchestration/DI
  edges; the correctness kernel remains input-deterministic in the stated design.

## 279a2-candidates — retained for deeper trace

- **role-name capture:** marker-gated syntax but permanent, unmarked `__role`
  recognition may retroactively turn ordinary successful functions into probe/vouch
  authority without a loud failure.
- **PATH re-entry identity:** the per-run `dorc-sh` object is smoke-tested once, but
  authored PATH transforms can resolve a different same-named executable; absence
  safely yields 127, substitution does not.
- **relation composition:** `277` specifies a codomain, consumer map, and generator
  inventory but not how generator evidence composes, conflicts, or reaches one result.
- **two-binary floor:** differential binaries are strong calibration oracles but do not
  define the static acceptance algorithm, observation boundary, or above-floor
  semantics the new parser must implement.
- **pipefail/off-ramp fracture:** bare pipefail is legal dorc-lang while the portable
  floor deliberately rejects it; strip does not rewrite it and the gate idiom is only
  blessed, not required.
- **unsafe scope:** bare `sh` is called the only permanent hatch, but the ruled escape
  semantics apply to oracle-body lines; book `sh -c` is deliberately decomposed through
  the stdlib oracle, leaving the admin-side opaque-region need unnamed.
- **trap control flow:** treating registration as an ordinary opaque state wall does not
  model the handler's later implicit edges, status, or mutations.
- **observe-set completeness:** `:?` asserts that a read observes X, not that X is the
  only input to every produced channel. `275` nevertheless treats the union of such
  marks as a complete per-channel backing set whose negative space licenses freshness
  and cross-context transport. Test whether this is a new unflagged faultless fold.

## 279a2-withdrawn-so-far

- **site-keying conflict:** `277`'s “probe keying” wording is compatible with a
  coordinate-indexed fact/probe map while retaining site-keyed result records; no
  demonstrated breach of `inv-site-keyed-results`.
- **capture line deletion:** the package explicitly records that eliding the assignment
  would unbind runtime consumers and routes disposition to block-context planning. It
  does not claim that executable substitution is already solved.
- **sudo policy fidelity:** the examples understate platform policy variance, but the
  authored argparse/decline design degrades unknown shapes to walls. This is a stdlib
  cost and coverage problem unless a mechanism bypass is found.
- **accepted survival unsoundness:** no bypass around `--risk-faultless-skips` was found
  in selector separation itself. Re-objecting to the already-recorded survival bargain
  would merely repeat a settled decision.
