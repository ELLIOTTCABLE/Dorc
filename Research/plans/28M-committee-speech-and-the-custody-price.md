## §1. Banked method (four tools; human-directed banking, 2026-07-29)

- **tool-purified-world** — when a hole confounds several dimensions (target-identity,
  version, authorship, opinion), construct the maximally-purified counterexample: pin every
  confound equal (same tool, same host, same version) and see whether the hole survives.
  Whatever survives is the core; whatever vanished belongs to the pinned dimension's own
  subdivision. Used here: the hole survived with nothing left in it but two *opinions*
  ("is this residue disregardable"), proving the core is identity-free and `sub-different-tool`
  is separable (MH2-owned). Trigger: any hole that resists solution because it is secretly
  several holes.
- **tool-opinion-gradient** [ACKED as valuable-but-imprecise] — within authored speech,
  distinguish *grammar-tier* claims (peel position, argv arity: opinions rarely diverge;
  near-factual; cheap to trust across authors; worth MECHANICAL COHERENCE CHECKS — the
  peel-coherence precedent) from *judgment-tier* claims (residue disregardability,
  convergence-adequacy: designed to diverge; NEVER pooled across authors). Not structural,
  never provable — a design-attention allocator: put checking where opinions converge,
  consent/refusal where they diverge. Spreads attention efficiently over
  gradual-enhancement territory.
- **tool-structural-chimera-immunity** [ACKED as duplicable mechanism] — the `safe-across`
  pattern: a claim that lives INSIDE the unit it warrants travels with it and can never be
  orphaned or recontextualized by someone else's later speech. Generalization (human's
  spelling): instead of enlarging units (functions → files-as-speakers) and binding claims
  to the larger unit, DUPLICATE the claim into each current-unit (function) that depends on
  it, and have the engine REQUIRE the copies cohere. "No more cross-unit speech
  *implication* — only cross-unit speech *checking*." Mechanical, trivial, provable; costs
  cargo-cult duplication and noisy happy-path errors. A toolbox item, not a default.
- **tool-composition-surface-decomposition** — the method of this sitting, for future
  design corners: (1) enumerate where the ENGINE actually assembles multi-utterance
  sentences (composition surfaces), never user stories; (2) test each surface against
  existing walls (monologue-by-construction? single-occupancy slot? day-one-priced
  cross-X surface? consent-gated?); (3) name the residue and its sole consumers; (4) assign
  each subdivision to **A** (flag/consent gate, double-ended where blast-radius crosses
  authors), **B** (named, bounded, single-author attribution), or **C** (seek closed-form;
  failing that, a standing review law). Triggers: any multi-author speech-composition
  question; any "hole" that won't reduce; and MANDATORILY at introduction of any new tier
  that consumes cross-member composition (see §5 law).

## §2. The hole, stated once (speech-act form; the purified world)

Dorc's safety rests on three properties: every license traces to utterances with a named
speaker; each utterance is consumed in the role its speaker's contract prices; composition
across speakers crosses only surfaces someone staked a name on (reverse-DNS kinds). The
FAMILY violates the third permanently: its name is forced by the world's command word — a
commons, nothing to own, no self-naming act available — yet it is the engine's principal
composition surface (members compose into one collective description; words into one
dialect; positions into one book-wide answer). Multi-author families therefore generate
engine-assembled composite judgments that no one uttered, that a participant may have
explicitly REFUSED to utter (a decline is load-bearing speech), whose components' meanings
are non-local in other authors' later choices (committee speech is RETROACTIVE: an
unchanged at-most claim, calibrated against its author's own declines, liberalizes when
someone else's blessed override flips the decline), and whose failures every participant
can truthfully repudiate. The bought-unsoundness contract cannot price a sentence with no
author; the kind hatch cannot cover a slot that cannot refuse occupancy without severing
name-binding. Sound multi-author families do not exist; only fences do.

Why the kind hatch works where this fails [ACKED framing]: reverse-DNS buys rare +
attributable + repairable-at-one-site, by SINGLE OCCUPANCY (one resolver, duplicates
refused; one surveyor per `only`). It converts multi-author danger into single-author
trust. Family names fail all three legs: collision is the ecosystem's success condition,
no norm is violated, nothing can be renamed. The end-state that re-applies the kind move
to vocabulary: **kind-level token registration** (words move into the owned namespace;
already queued as the registered-verdict-selector dispatch, `analysis/CLAUDE.md`).

## §3. The subdivision map (the walls, then the residue)

Set aside first: **sub-different-tool** [TYPED] — every instance downstream of "the
descriptions refer to different actual tools" (platform variants, versions). MH2-owned;
its re-entry trigger sharpened to "the first real stdlib fork" (`28O` banked the same
direction). And **sub-unit-custody-general** [TYPED set-aside] — whole-file-as-speaker as
a GENERAL mechanism: majority-language solution, gradual-enhancement-hostile in a world of
unevenly-attended third-party descriptors; not adopted as a rule (but see the narrow
disqualifier in §4).

Walls — provably not subject, each with its existing mechanism:

- **wall-no-plurality** — dual-live bodies are unreachable without an explicit admin
  speech-act (stage E's refusal + blessing). Whole population of never-blessing admins: ∅.
- **wall-verdict-tier-sovereignty** — verdict → guard/elide at own sites is a monologue;
  per-position sovereign after D+E (declines never fall through). The purified example's
  LICENSE structure is sound here: one opinion governs each site, admin-chosen. ∅.
- **wall-no-travel-members** — a family with no disturbs/lend_map/enter loaded has no
  travel-capable speech; committee-immune regardless of plurality; detectable at load. ∅.
- **wall-cross-family-was-always-priced** — composed probes (per-channel coverage rules)
  and wrapper↔guest (27C's double-ended consent) were multi-author from day one and carry
  day-one contracts. In the purified world the wrapper members' load-bearing claims are
  grammar-tier (~SUSPECT, see tool-opinion-gradient) and the one load-bearing cross-member
  coherence (peel position) is statically checked. Existing-A.
- **wall-self-vouches-travel-with-bodies** — `safe-across` is chimera-immune by
  construction (the warrant lives in the warranted text). ∅. The mechanism to duplicate
  elsewhere (§1 third tool).
- **wall-kind-owner-single-occupancy** — resolve/reach/stored-in: existing-B. Cross-author
  fact co-reference through kinds stays under the standing `fence-divergent-meaning`
  documented cap (one added sentence there when settled: within-name divergence is the
  sneakier face of the same cap).
- **wall-steady-state** — the residue below is consulted only when a wall actually runs:
  drifted days, under the flag.

Residue — the license-bearing committee surfaces, both feeding ONE consumer:

- **res-disturbs-decline-calibration** — an at-most footprint's precision is calibrated
  against its own author's declines; a blessed cross-author verdict-override retroactively
  liberalizes the unchanged claim.
- **res-dialect-word-pooling** — selector vocabularies keyed by the family-name commons let
  one live body's tokens be read against another's backings.

Both live entirely inside the survival tier (`--risk-faultless-skips`) — the corner already
concentrated, bounded, and explicitly bought.

## §4. How the residue closes

Both §3 residues are closed by keying the sparing dialect to ONE speaker closure and
reading it at the backing's frame (`30J:rul-dialect-is-the-live-speakers-at-the-backing-frame`):
one definition is live per frame, so one speaker's vocabulary is consulted and the
composite "A's word ≠ B's word" is never minted. No fence over the family is needed.
Rejected on the way there: the DefId-by-content lean (bytes are the wrong proxy — a
one-arm fork severs a genuinely shared language; names too coarse, bytes too fine;
only custody speaks). Escape from any cross-speaker shape stays native and
speech-shaped: fork-the-file = taking custody of the whole utterance
(vendoring-with-custody, `28K` §3).

**Standing review law [PROPOSED]:** every future tier that consumes cross-member
composition passes §1's decomposition at design time — the walls above inventory TODAY'S
consumers only; the hole's general form re-opens with any new composite consumer.

Aid-plane rider: a chimeric family's philosophical incoherence (site 5 elided liberally,
site 9 walled conservatively) is real and confusing — hints/narration under the kWARN
weld, never licensure.

## §5. The coherent design (grades; unratified items marked)

   other respects indistinguishable from books; candidate fifth exclusion-check axis
   "the other home" (relocate the code between book and oracle-file) [human-suggested;

5. Emission: licensure never rides runtime name-resolution; pin-by-definition-bytes;

## §6. The price (human-demanded honest pricing; general ops terms)

**Completely screwed — states with real value to somebody, unrepresentable by design:**

- **price-patch-author** — the overlay/inheritance archetype: the person who maintains a
  small delta against upstream description (the Ansible role-override user, the nixpkgs
  overlay user, the Chef wrapper-cookbook user). The state they want — "upstream's family
  + my one arm, as one mutually-trusting first-class description, without my owning the
  whole file" — cannot exist: its soundness would require a sentence nobody can be made
  to have said. What they get instead: a member added in their own closure speaks as a
  stranger — its claims are read in the upstream speaker's vocabulary (`30J` §6.5), its
  own marks spare nothing until their closure qualifies — or fork-with-custody (own the
  whole utterance and its rot). Entire ops cultures are built on the refused workflow.
- **price-union-of-strangers** — the admin with two half-coverage community oracles for
  one tool who expects the union (plugin-ecosystem intuition; pre-28K first-match gave an
  ACCIDENTAL partial union). Deliberately unrepresentable (`rej-argparse-fusion-forever`,
  `rej-decline-fallthrough-cascade`): one body answers per site; union requires a human
  hand-merge, i.e. custody.
- **price-forbidden-to-fork** — orgs whose policy forbids forking vendored third-party
  code (consume-verbatim-only): custody is their only extension path and it is closed to
  them. Niche; subsumes into price-patch-author.

Multi-file packages pay nothing: one entry file sourcing its parts is one speaker
(`28Q:syn-closure-is-the-speaker`).

**Rent climbed — representable, now cruftier/more annoying:**

- bottom-appenders [if positional ratified]: the stage-3 appended oracle licenses nothing
  above itself; move-it-up hint carries the recovery; USER_STORY's "append" wording is a
  human edit someday.
- regional-preference admins: inside the sanctioned subshell re-source the region's
  live definition's dialect governs (`30J` §12); a claim spelled in the ambient
  speaker's word collides there — extra guards on drifted days, why-chains explaining why.
- whole-file forkers: full value, plus the universal fork-maintenance rent (tracking
  upstream by hand).
- deliberate overriders: one `unset -f` ceremony line per replaced member, plus the
  refusal nag until spelled (`28K:res-overlay-primacy-tax`, unchanged).
- in-book vocabulary prototypers: kind-owner experiments must move to a loaded file
  (refuse-with-notice).
- plan reviewers: fatter artifacts (hoisted pinned definitions, provenance blocks,
  contest complaints) — mitigated by attention-honesty defaults.
- stdlib maintainers: support noise from partial-override users discovering that their
  own marks spare nothing until their closure qualifies (`30J` §5).

**Who pays nothing:** the 20-line-book, stdlib-only, never-blessing admin — the
overwhelming majority — every wall in §3 is ∅ for them.

**The trade, named:** Dorc declines the overlay culture at the sharp tier. Where other
ecosystems let you modify and combine strangers' descriptions without ownership (and
diffuse the blame when the merge misbehaves — nobody can debug a five-layer hiera merge
either), Dorc's sharp tier answers only to custody: speak whole, or the engine composes
nothing in your name. The verdict/guard tier — most of the product's value — remains open
to everyone at every rung. That is the price of never minting a sentence with no speaker;
it is paid mostly by the archetype that other ecosystems subsidize by making their
composites nobody's fault.

## §8. The contest, lowered: composition, custody, and what survives (design sitting, 2026-07-31)

## §11. Cross-family registration (the one vocabulary question not in `30J`)

Selector-granular sparing is the survival tier's value: without any token vocabulary,
every same-kind-same-entity comparison collides and drifted days re-check everything
(safe, less attention saved). With a vocabulary, the engine must judge, from two
STRINGS, whether a wall's at-most claim `@a` touches a protected fact `@b` — and strings
cannot distinguish "two aspects of one thing" (spare is right) from "two names for one
state" (spare silently under-executes). Within one speaker the per-speaker dialect
(`30J` §12) makes that the speaker's own lexical discipline. ACROSS families it is
nobody's:

```sh
# the postgres family DESCRIBES two aspects:
#   pg_isready ...      : sm.dorc.Service:postgres@accepting
#   pgrep -x postgres   : sm.dorc.Service:postgres@running      (process-up, narrow)
# a stranger's pgbouncer oracle claims, meaning "the whole service lifecycle":
#   reload) printf '%s\n' postgres    : disturbs sm.dorc.Service:postgres@running
```

Claim `@running` vs backing `@accepting`: both words in the postgres speaker's dialect,
distinct ⇒ spare — while the reload genuinely bounced connection-acceptance. The
stranger never surveyed the family's word-meanings; nobody lied; the elision that
needed to fall, stood. Today this rests on `fence-divergent-meaning`'s documented cap.

**registration** — whether deliberate cross-family co-reference can exist at all:
the kind-owner curates the kind's words; co-reference iff BOTH words are registered;
unregistered keeps the collide tier. Spelling lean: registration-by-owner-use over the
owner's closure (no new construct); mark-verb fallback. Design-shaped, UNRULED, build
unscheduled; `30J` §6.5 is its pointer. Sibling item, the human's to mint or decline:
the KNOBS commons-axis name (`28K:res-knobs-entry-owed` — collision-as-success commons
↔ owned adjudicated speech).
