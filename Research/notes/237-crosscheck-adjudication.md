## Post-adjudication corrections (human engagement, 2026-07-02 — same day)

- **The attention-recovery claim on convergence-3 is refuted.** The human's argument, accepted by
  the conductor: approval happens BEFORE the initial application, and no re-observation scheme

  under the no-hidden-danger doctrine it must be presented in full ("all of this might happen")
  regardless of mechanism. The only alternative shape — pause at the wall and present a fresh
  partial plan — trades the user's attention for their *time* (sitting through the mutative
  prefix, which is the slow part in the real world), per-wall. So: re-observation does NOT
  restore approval-time attention. What survives of it: (a) execution shape — one batched,
  parallel re-probe wave vs a serial per-site guard-tail; (b) a shorter post-wall *artifact*
  (file-form aesthetics, true elisions downstream of a verified wall); (c) observation-grade
  apply-reports. Both agents oversold this; the adjudication initially under-discounted it.
- **Wall-density sizing caveat (conductor, prompted by the human's challenge):** the barrier's
  benefit scales with the length of the post-wall region, and under the human's own ~90/10 prior
  (opaques scattered through real books, not singular) epochs are short, waves are frequent, and
  per-wall round-trips eat the benefit. Hoisted re-verification dominates per-site guards only in
  the rare-wall regime; guards degrade gracefully with density. Any design round must cost-model
  this FIRST.
- **The placement-spectrum reframe (human):** don't architect this as "walls" — per-site guard ↔
  hoisted-to-after-the-wall wave is a *placement spectrum* for the same read-only re-verification
