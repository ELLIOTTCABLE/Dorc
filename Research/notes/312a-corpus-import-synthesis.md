## § 0-the-phase-and-its-conduct

The 311 model is held as if it were the final types. The work pushes it onto concrete ops
habits one at a time and cards only where it strains: which party can know a thing at all,
which can reasonably know it, what Dorc does when nobody can, and where a claim's danger flows.
Output per strain: leads and wants, never spellings; seat requirements, never API. Bound: short
of API design and difficult strawmen.

Conduct the human typed (2026-09-08): grounding is plain book-sh ops habits and `GOTCHAS.md`,
never Dorc-spelled sh; the model's response is prose in 311 vocabulary; seat spellings that
fall out are recorded without weight, never ruled, so splitting and merging stay fluid; one
record per exercise in `312a-exercises/`, not durable; `GOTCHAS.md` is authored freely now and
chopped later into a frontier file and an extras file; the singular Fable-subagent
authorization is the conductor's, best spent on medium synthesis over ten to twelve documents.

Alignment principle (**[HUMAN]**): blast radius aligns with the gradual-enhancement axis (new
user ⇒ low power, low blast radius; experienced ⇒ high power, high blast radius) and with
countability: high-blast-radius seats must be few, so they can be stdlib-owned and exhaustively
tested. "Stdlib" in this phase glosses "only the most powerful users; cannot be made less
dangerous."

## § 6-findings-and-thoughts

- `fnd-every-dissolved-design-was-forced` (+SURE) — all sixteen dissolved or re-litigated
  designs had a recorded concrete forcing case; none was whim. Ten still land safely under 311
  with no action; the rest are re-acks, not contradictions. No lesson was found dropped
  outright; the nearest is `30J`'s P2 gate, an incentive rule rather than a soundness rule.
- `fnd-policy-dependence-is-the-recurring-shape` (~SUSPECT it generalizes) — APT Post-Invoke
  hooks, sudoers matching the guest command, sudo's `env_keep` and `always_set_home`,
  unattended-upgrades: a tool's or wrapper's behaviour depends on host-local policy the author
  cannot hold and the admin wrote. The recurring resolution is one move made three times: the
  author declares the default; the oracle reads the policy in the denoted context and declines
  on departure; the admin's seat overrides. Deployment knowledge becomes a measurement the
  author can author.
- `fnd-convert-to-read-is-the-cheap-rung` (~SUSPECT as a general ladder) — for several holes the
  tool can simply be asked (pipx prints its store; apt-config prints its hooks; `sudo -l`
  prints its policy; git prints its config paths). Asking costs a probe and forfeits static
  reasoning; declaring the rule costs authoring. A two-rung ladder per hole, and the rungs sort
  onto the enhancement axis by themselves.
- `fnd-declared-by-column-is-the-first-draft` (+SURE) — `311` § 2.10's declared-by column
  already assigns every positive claim to a party. Assignment is an audit of that column
  against can-reasonably-know and blast radius, not a fresh table.
- `fnd-leverage-and-blast-radius-are-one-number` (+SURE; the alignment is the human's) — a
  per-mSort claim amortizes across every book and bites every book. Countability follows: few
  such seats, all stdlib, all tested.
- `fnd-witnessed-versus-declared-sorts-the-answers` (+SURE by
  `26Ob:ack-guards-reach-elisions-witness`) — SAME from measured mTokens re-read at standup is
  witnessed; SAME or DISJOINT from a warrant alone is declared; the survival tier was always
  declared. Every card carries this bit.
- `fnd-known-unspoken-and-unknown-repair-differently` (+SURE of the text; ~SUSPECT of the intent) —
  known-unspoken can be lifted by the footprint side's finished definition; unknown only by the
  missing warrant. Which bottoms have a nearest person differs, and § 3.2 of 311 should say
  whether the asymmetry is deliberate.
- `fnd-admin-seat-is-owed` (+SURE) — by typed ruling (§ 3) and by every exercise so far
  (overrides; sudoers; host pairing). Whether it is a book-side declaration, a lint
  acknowledgement, or a posture flag is the assignment work's question, not this document's.
- `fnd-nobody-at-granularity-widens` (+SURE from `30T`) — a hole with an empty can-know set at
  grain g is filled at the coarsest grain that has a knower, and collides below it.
- `fnd-was-true-then-needs-a-column` (~SUSPECT) — honest claims outrun by a newer binary or a
  renamed key are not perishing (within-book time); MH2's deferred layer owns the mechanism;
  the cards need the column regardless.
- `fnd-measure-before-assuming-walls` (+SURE; `27B:finding-premise-unmeasured`) — "sudo is a
  permanent wall" was never measured. Assumptions about which habits are common should be
  measured before they shape seats.
- `fnd-granularity-incentive-in-one-line` (`23M`) — apt and a vendor tool both writing
  `/var/lib/dpkg/status`: a file-grained claim over-walls, a package-grained claim is precise.
  Same tool, two claim granularities, two outcomes.
- `fnd-vacuous-bottom-and-paranoid-authors` — a detached body once read as clean because
  vacuous-bottom looked like "nothing upstream mutated this" (`16P` DP-8): known-unspoken must never
  read as safe-to-spare. An executed ufw strawman's own defensive sanitiser produced a false
  converged verdict (`151` X4): even a careful author needs a mechanical net, which bears on
  which seats get one.
