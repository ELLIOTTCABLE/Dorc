# 27x-strawmen-topology — the trichotomy candidates under five hostile real-world tools

AI-authored (Fable, design-rubber-duck sitting, 2026-07-10), at the human's direction:
"choose 5 non-trivial-for-those-shapes real-world examples and work through them one at
a time," as the survival test before committing to the task-3 (adj-trichotomy-spelling)
direction. Everything here is STRAWMAN-tier: no syllable is settled, the member name
`__lives_at` is a placeholder, and these files are design exercises, not fixtures —
they are never executed by anything.

## The two shapes under test

- **Shape A — the minted static clause** (the demoted fallback): a free-standing,
  machine-read, per-(kind × axis) declaration, illustratively
  `: sm.dorc.Package user=invariant`. Author asserts the classification directly.
- **Shape D — address-derived topology** (the current primary; see the `271` ledger's
  sitting entry): a per-KIND family member whose body spells WHERE THE KIND'S STATE
  LIVES in ordinary sh; the engine derives the classification by dependence-analysis
  of the traced body under ρ (user-axis-owned inputs: `$HOME`, `$USER`, `$LOGNAME`,
  blessed `$(id -un)`/`$(id -u)`/`$(whoami)` captures). **Carve in force
  (never-derive-separation):** the derivation may yield the identification license
  (invariance) and per-value KEYING; it may never yield cross-context DISJOINTNESS —
  that would be separation-by-address-inequality, the naive-string sin.

Emission-marking note: `__lives_at` emissions are deliberately UN-marked (no `: file`),
on the `resolve()` precedent — a role whose co-domain is fixed by its own contract
needs no annotation slot, and here the bare co-domain is fence-enforcing: what carries
no kind cannot be intersected with coordinates, which is exactly the co-reference
fence (addresses are consumed for the dependence bit ONLY). This is a deliberate carve
from `24G` §5's emission-without-typing-means-nothing law, mirroring resolve's.

Calling convention (strawman): `$1` = entity, `$2` = selector (absent ⇒ whole-entity).

## Index, with one-line verdicts

1. `01-systemd-user-units.sh` — system vs `--user` units. Verdict: kinds are the
   TOPOLOGY UNIT; mixed-topology tools split kinds via argv-driven binding; D makes
   each half derivable, A must assert it blind. Bonus: the exhaustiveness knife
   NARROWS under the co-reference fence (approximate invariant addresses are fine;
   only a FORGOTTEN user-scoped store bites).
2. `02-git-config-scopes.sh` — `--system`/`--global`/`--local`. Verdict: address-shaped
   entities absorb most "sensitivity" into the value plane (ρ); the topology machinery
   is only needed for LOGICAL entities; everything address-shaped reduces to ONE
   bootstrap declaration on the file kind.
3. `03-postgres-peer-auth.sh` — the r25 permanent wall. Verdict: referent-topology
   (invariant, TRUE) is orthogonal to ACCESS-gating (peer auth, per-probe, enforced by
   rc-reality, imp-1); the invariance license composes gracefully with denial.
4. `04-homebrew-prefix.sh` — `$(brew --prefix)` dynamic root. Verdict: D's dynamic
   arms are CONSUMERS of the task-7 capture-claim vocabulary (a captured address needs
   an axis-independence value-bound, or it degrades to silence-floor); Shape A scores
   its one honest win here (cheap assertion where D needs unbuilt machinery).
5. `05-docker-rootless.sh` — rootless vs system daemon. Verdict: A is inexpressible,
   D can spell the client's real selection logic — and the system-daemon host is a
   LIVE COUNTEREXAMPLE that upgrades never-derive-separation from precaution to
   demonstrated necessity (alice and root genuinely share one daemon there).
