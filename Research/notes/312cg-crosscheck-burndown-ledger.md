## § 4-corrections-to-312c

- `cor-flat-default-is-the-floor-for-positional-catalogs` (conductor; ~SUSPECT; unreviewed by
  the human) — `312c` thread 2 called the wide reading of the flat default a defect and its
  narrowing the repair. That was too strong. A positional catalog (ufw and iptables rule
  numbers, a crontab's lines, an array in a config file) renumbers its siblings on an entry
  write, so an entry write there does perish sibling resolutions (`311s` cluster B9). The wide
  reading is the correct floor for such catalogs. The repair is a describer's declaration that a
  flat catalog's entries are independently addressed, which is 312's spelling; the lost elision
  below a drifted `apt-get install` stands as the cost until it exists. Thread 2 therefore joins
  § 3 as held, with this correction attached.
