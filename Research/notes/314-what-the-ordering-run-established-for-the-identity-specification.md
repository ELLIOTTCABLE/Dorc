## § 0-what-was-asked

**[TYPED]** 2026-10-04, the run's remit:

- Find the order in which the design work on the identity, relation, and effects model must be
  done so that the design firms and narrows monotonically. A question is ready to be put only
  when it is well defined and rests on no other open question.
- The order is to be measured with the checker and attacked by adversarial review, not planned
  from priors.
- Vocabulary is a first-class product. A fundamental object of the model that the corpus never
  named clearly gets a sitting of its own: explained, acked, and durably named. That sitting is
  a prerequisite of any ruling about the object's behaviour.

The run did not produce a measured order. § 1 says why that was not possible by the route
taken; § 2 says what ordering did follow.

## § 1-what-a-checker-can-and-cannot-order

- `ord-a-sweep-gives-couplings-and-no-order` (+SURE, argued; all three lanes of the first
  review reached it) — encode each open question as a set of alternative predicates, sweep
  every combination, and call Q dependent on R when two answers of R leave Q different
  non-empty sets of surviving answers. That relation is symmetric by construction. Take an
  answer of Q in one set and not the other, and any answer in the other set: R's surviving sets
  at those two answers differ and are non-empty. So every edge has its reverse, no edge joins
  two clusters, and any order read off the result is the order a script walked it in.
- `ord-a-coupling-is-not-one-sitting` (+SURE) — two coupled questions can be put in either
  order. What the coupling requires is that the one put first is put with the table of what
  each of its answers leaves the other.
- `ord-direction-is-statability` (~SUSPECT as method) — the only notion with a direction that
  the run found is whether a question can be stated: its terms have names, and the positions it
  is put inside are chosen. This is the remit's third point applied to every question. The
  run's attempt to compute it from model text did not hold (§ 6).

## § 2-ordering-that-follows

Each is a precedence between kinds of work. None is a schedule.

- `ord-name-a-relation-before-any-choice-that-reads-it` (+SURE as a rule; found as a violation
  of the run's own draft order by all three lanes of the second review) — a choice among
  readings of what a claim covers, or of how a closure composes, reads the relation "a write to
  this can change that" and the relation of one thing being inside another. Naming those cannot
  be left to the later sitting that chooses each relation's statements. All naming of the world
  side comes before the first choice that reads any of it.
- `ord-confirm-first-only-what-nothing-contests` (+SURE) — a list of standing rulings confirmed
  at the start must leave out any ruling that is the latest position on a matter a later sitting
  puts as open. Confirming it there answers the later sitting before its terms are named.
- `ord-instants-before-truth-sentences` (+SURE of the count, ~SUSPECT of the consequence) —
  whether a key's reach can differ between two lines is decided before any statement's truth
  sentence is ruled, or ported into a new document. In `specs/311-identity.assay.md`, `reaches`
  is read by 19 of the 28 truth predicates (every one that has a body), by all 10 laws that
  take the truths as one premise, and by 6 of the 9 holes. `312f` § 13.2 already found that no
  world can reuse a key while `reaches` is timeless. The foray's remit asks for time and
  ordering "right from the start" (`313e` § 1, **[TYPED]** there).
- `ord-the-wrongness-criterion-before-any-licence-result` (+SURE) — "the end state differs from
  the bare book's" and "a line that ran touched what the fact rested on" are different criteria.
  A model with two evolutions from one start has worlds where the at-most claim and the vouch
  are true, nothing interferes, and the end state differs; with the adequacy of the vouch false
  it has the converged-is-not-no-op case that `TODO.md` holds. Every other measurement of the
  run fixed the interference reading. So no result about the licence carries over to the
  end-state reading, and the criterion is chosen before such results are relied on.
- `ord-whether-a-facts-dependence-is-a-closure-before-the-flag` (+SURE of the rows, one lane) —
  `313a` § 2.3 holds open whether a fact's dependence is itself a closure. The question of which
  licences need the flag turns on it. In the licence model, the human's latest typed line on the
  read side (both ends' closures, `312cg:391-394`) together with the latest on the flag (a
  flag-free floor, `313e:24-27`) passes in 0 of 48 profiles while the vouch counts as a negative
  that closes over an unclosed world, and in 44 of 48 when it does not (four and five atoms).
  The coupling of the ends to the flag exists only under the first counting.
- `ord-what-a-claim-covers-is-put-with-what-disjoint-must-mean` (~SUSPECT; a coupling, stated
  as corrected by all three lanes) — under a verb's at-most claim that covers the line's own
  writes, the sparing law has a wrong spare with every statement true in 36 of 192 profiles;
  the specification's holes are worlds of this kind. Under a claim that covers everything that
  happens, it has none. Under the first reading, what a right DISJOINT must mean depends on
  whether the one-thing-beneath shape is in scope of the law: in scope, at least neither thing
  inside the other; held outside, as the specification holds it today, two different things is
  enough. Under the first reading too, a finished record demanded only across sorts
  (`30U:15-19`) passes only where the reader's end is closed as well (`315` § 1.8).
- `ord-two-sorts-waits-on-no-sense-of-disjoint` (+SURE of the rows; a second-round lane re-ran
  them under each sense) — what the comparison answers for two sorts nobody related (`315`
  § 1.17) can be put before what a right DISJOINT means. Under each of three senses of
  DISJOINT, two answers fail the walk's DISJOINT law in every profile, because two keys of two
  sorts can reach one thing: always-disjoint, which is what the built comparison does
  (`spike/crates/core/src/coord.rs`), and disjoint where the written sort is finished (`30U`),
  with the specification's truth for the finished record; `30U`'s own wider sentence was not
  measured. Known-unspoken unless a key chain separates them passes, in fewer contexts as the
  sense narrows.
- `ord-what-disjoint-means-is-put-with-the-thing-beneath` (~SUSPECT; the walk at depth two, rows
  equal at four to six atoms) — who says that nothing sits beneath two separated things is put
  with what a right DISJOINT means (`315` § 3 item 1 is the closure that would say it). If it
  means two different things, the shape can stay open with the law asked outside it, take that
  closure, or be a stated horizon; if neither thing inside the other, the closure or the
  horizon; if no shared part, the horizon only. The horizon sentence tested (no thing has two
  holders; a key with no parent key reaches a thing nothing holds) is the first lane's, and no
  source states it. A deeper walk could move the closure's row.
- `ord-names-that-move-wait-on-instants` (+SURE) — nothing about a contained write and its
  store's naming can be checked until a key's reach can vary. Once it can, one result needs no
  sitting: checking only the fact's key fails in every profile where names move (an earlier
  line re-points the writing line's key onto the fact's thing), and checking every compared key
  passes. Two lineages found it independently. The specification already checks both.

## § 3-what-candidate-rulings-do-in-the-specifications-own-encoding

From a scratch compile of the specification and its shared halves, outside `specs/`, each
ruling added as a premise on copies of the commands and never as a fact; 180 s per command, the
scopes the specification states.

- `abl-no-hole-is-a-case-of-another` (+SURE of the rows) — with any one hole's shape forbidden,
  every other hole's witness stays satisfiable (72 of 72). The nine holes are nine shapes.
- `abl-the-cell-and-composite-sentences-close-their-holes` — with `DeclaresCell.isTrue` and
  `DeclaresComposite.isTrue` given the sentence that their things are one thing, both witnesses
  have no instance, and `law_compare_same_is_sound` and `law_same_is_sound` have no
  counterexample with those exclusions dropped, premise twins satisfiable. Two DISJOINT laws
  that also exclude the composite hole timed out under the ruling. A review lane showed the
  specification's own uniqueness fact with the cell hole as a premise is equivalent, so the
  sentence's content is needed either way and who holds it is the open part.
- `abl-total-closures-discharge-the-third-thing-hole` — with the finished record and the closed
  may-read set reading the transitive closure of `affects`, `law_sparing_is_sound` has no
  counterexample without the third-thing exclusion, twin satisfiable; the shape itself stays
  satisfiable. With `affects` transitive in every world the shape has no instance and the law
  result is the same. Asking the law outside the shape, as the specification does, also passes:
  the one-step reading is not refuted, it needs that premise.
- `abl-a-stronger-store-closure-closes-nothing` — three readings of a stronger
  `:aliases-nothing-else` leave the one-thing-beneath, world-scoped-top, and off-catalog shapes
  satisfiable and the laws that exclude them failing. In the instances the separation comes from
  `:guarantees-unique-name`, with no store closure in force at all.
- `abl-one-exclusion-was-not-needed-at-its-scope` — `law_sparing_is_sound` has no counterexample
  with the composite exclusion dropped, and the composite shape coexists with its premise there.
- `312f` § 12 records a test of its own framing: three or four rulings on what the world
  relations mean should collapse most effect-side holes. Of the four shapes that framing lists
  under its second stand-in, the rulings tried closed one. ~SUSPECT these are the kind of
  ruling that test means.

## § 4-praxis-for-measuring-a-design-question

Each was learned from a result of the run that a reviewer's counter-model overturned.

- `prx-an-answer-carries-its-sources-premises` — an answer is the position its source states,
  premises included. Where the specification asks a law outside a held hole, that exclusion is
  part of the specification's answer. Asked without it, three answers read as failing that pass
  with it.
- `prx-one-answer-changes-one-sentence` — an answer that changes two sentences at once measures
  the bundle.
- `prx-an-answer-must-be-able-to-fail` — an answer whose truth sentence is the law's own
  conclusion passes everywhere and frees every other question, by construction. The sign is a
  region where the law is green and its kills have no instance. A profile counts as passing
  only where a kill is live.
- `prx-invented-answers-stay-out-of-the-tables` — two answers no source holds carried ten of
  the first sweep's edges.
- `prx-one-position-is-one-question` — a single position split across three questions shows as
  mutual dependence among its own parts.
- `prx-four-atoms-is-not-five` — a pair of closures that each look one level down passed at
  four atoms and failed at five and six: two roots, a member in each, one thing beneath both
  members. Headline rows are run at five at least.
- `prx-a-value-witness-decides-no-hard-result` — one value check forced an answer in the first
  sweep. `313e` § 1 already records a mechanical value yardstick as nacked.
- `prx-no-shared-model-is-not-independence` — two questions never swept in one model have no
  measured relation. Most pairs were in that state.
- `prx-ask-reviewers-for-things-that-run` — the review forms that found real faults were: a
  counter-model with its command and row; a walk, in which the reviewer fixes one answer per
  question and steps through the proposed order until a question cannot be stated or no table
  gives the chosen answer a consequence; and an order violation cited by sitting and term. The
  review copy had the authors' own doubts removed and kept the list of what was not measured.
- `prx-a-status-is-checked-before-it-is-a-premise` — a line handed to a builder as a fixed
  point is first checked against its ledger's own definition of its tag.

## § 5-what-bears-on-313

- `key-any-definition-moves-every-key` (+SURE; measured on scratch copies with nothing solved,
  and read from the key's code) — a row's key hashes every definition of its document. A ruling
  entered as added units (a new predicate, a law that takes it as a premise, the twin) moved
  125 of the lock's 125 keys. The same law with the ruling written inline and no new predicate
  moved 63, the rows of the law module. An edit to one truth predicate moved 125. So each ruling
  that adds a definition costs a full re-solve of its document, on the present document or a
  new one. --WONDER one document per tier, each opening the one before, would keep earlier
  keys; not measured. This is a cost of `313` § 0's constraint, not a breach of it: a moved key
  is not a moved result.
- `law-ten-of-fourteen-share-one-premise` (+SURE as parsed) — 10 of the 14 laws take all 28
  truth predicates through one conjoined premise; 9 of the 28 have an empty body.
- `313` § 1.2, measured: with the two table facts as one named premise that only table-reading
  commands state, the 36 of 63 law-module commands that read no table fell from 17 to 30 s to
  1 to 9 s on the sample, and every definite verdict equalled the lock. With the glucose solver
  as well, `law_region_disjoint_is_sound` and `law_compare_disjoint_is_sound` returned no
  counterexample inside 180 s, where the lock records timeouts at 1800 s. The store-on-the-chain
  sparing law still did not finish.

## § 6-what-did-not-hold

- The first sweep's clusters, its "root pair" of questions, and most of its forced and dead
  answers were products of its encodings and of the symmetric measure (§ 1, § 4).
- A second sweep computed which question's answers are written in terms of another's by
  following references in the model text. The result changed under an equivalent re-flowing of
  one predicate, and it is direction inside one encoding only.
- A proposed order of sittings in tiers was written from those tables and from reading. About
  20 of its 32 choices were placed by reading. It is not recorded here.
- Nothing measured chose between amending the mechanised specification in place, growing a new
  document and porting into it, and the floor-and-freeze fallback of `312f` § 12.
- Not modelled at all: which closures a describer can truthfully make from what they can see;
  wrappers and vantage; the route relation; walks deeper than two levels; books of more than
  two lines; any model holding both names that move and the licence.
