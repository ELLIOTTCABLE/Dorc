## §1 the deferral, and its reason

The curated/delicate why surfaces (default renders, ranking, registers,
staged search) are DEFERRED — no build this round `[TYPED]`. Ground: their
correctness criterion ("deeply effective for the derived goal") is unstatable
pre-user; the dag-explanation-ux finding stands that badly-curated filtering
is *more* damaging than showing everything; work here pre-user can only be a
boondoggle. The buildable/deferred split follows the TESTABILITY boundary: a
surface whose quality function is a census builds now (§5); a surface whose
quality function is taste waits for users.

## §2 posture (leans and rulings gathered; the render stays unwelded)

- rul-types-first-lossy-at-display `[TYPED]` — the reconstructed model fully
  and anally describes everything (temporality, provenance, quality,
  lossiness); every aid surface is lossy only at the last possible moment,
  projecting into layout structure when no other work remains; fence this by
  construction where cheap (layout types hold only encoder-emitted atoms;
  omissions are data — an exclusion ledger, so the absent-curation tier is
  literally the empty exclusion set).
- rul-first-person-register `[TYPED]` — the tool speaks as the human-through-
  the-tool: "I" wherever no more-correct register exists ("the oracle checked
  itself", "the author vouched"). The register is the terminal attribution
  link: a claim grounded in nobody else's speech is the tool's own, and the
  types must keep I-speech and quoted-speech distinct end-to-end.
- rul-intent-disambiguation-fails-fast `[TYPED]` — resolving what the user
  MEANT is never gradual-enhancement: ambiguous input (line specifiers
  included) stops and asks immediately, within the known-user-present
  windows; best-effort only where no clear disambiguation exists to offer.
- rul-why-never-summarizes-for-tidiness `[TYPED]` — the why surface exists
  precisely for the tidy-looking failure: everything proceeded to plan, the
  world presents clean, and the frame problem or referential agnosticism bit
  the user invisibly. A quiet-looking record never licenses curation-down;
  the invocation itself is evidence of subtle wrongness, the worst kind.
- rul-slurp-then-project `[TYPED posture; implementation deferred]` — ingest
  everything relevant onto one structure (receipt-graph walk included), do
  all investigation/rederivation/joining there, and only then swap postures
  into ripping/filtering/projecting. Receipts are never-append/write-only, so
  discovering later referencing receipts has no forward links and is
  lossy-or-slow; acceptable, deferred.
- rul-line-addresses-are-namespaced `[TYPED]` — minimum address is
  `file.sh:N`; the surface speaks in the namespace the user spoke in; where
  user-namespace and recorded-namespace agree the line is simply stated, and
  where the file has changed they cannot be perfectly matched and get
  explicit `né <oldline>` treatment. A fuller disambiguating specifier
  (on-disk vs seen-in-an-error historical; receipt-ID-addressable) is TBD.
- rul-stateful-narrowing-hard-gated `[TYPED]` — explore DETERMINISTIC first:
  identical reinvocation reproduces identical reconstruction, so a user's
  multi-step dig-dive re-enters by address, not by session state. kSTATE is
  explicitly acknowledged not to bind debugging/investigation surfaces — but
  ideally state stays unnecessary anyway; don't force a difficult UI to
  avoid it, don't reach for it lazily.
- rul-no-bisection-engine `[TYPED]` — the surface never models what the user
  has already seen and never narrows on their behalf ("it wasn't in the
  last-seen set, so…" is a footgun that hides what a tired, angry, fallible
  human missed). Projection rules stay simple, observable, global-esque,
  light-handed, predictable; never magic.
- lean-why-is-reached-second — strong intuition, not hard: the admin arrives
  with a specific wrong thing already identified (a logged error, a downed
  service) and asks about THAT; convergence is the remit, so the named line
  is the entry. Corollaries: effect/data-flow unrolling is load-bearing (the
  named line is the first thread, not the cause), and the addressless
  total view is the BACK-OUT surface when the first pull catches nothing —
  secondary but real.
- lean-chronology-not-yet-law — "declare one frame, then uniform past tense"
  is a compression valid in single-moment cells, not law; temporality is
  per-datum in the model and the framing stays open to experimentation.
- rul-remedies-may-fork `[TYPED]` — committee speech (synthesized conclusions
  requiring multiple humans' claims, e.g. `disturbance_reaches` across
  kinds) yields two-pronged leverage points: "fix A's claim or reopen B's
  definition, depending on which is wrong — I cannot tell."
- rul-presentation-register-split `[TYPED]` — `--json` owns premeditated
  power-filtering; therefore the default text surface optimizes READABILITY
  slightly over grep-friendliness (its pipe-consumer has already read our
  format and wants less of the same), while keeping locality and stable
  labels so `grep -C` habits work. Single-line-per-record is dead as the
  default; it survives as a `--oneline`-style option (a daily-driver
  constituency exists).
- rul-orderings-owe-a-lever `[TYPED]` — the moment two sortings exist
  (trust-first, program-lexical, …), a user-control lever is owed: defaults
  may differ per invocation surface, but every ordering is available on
  every surface. Discipline against the git-log-help-page fate: a lever
  ships only as a projection parameter that already exists for another
  surface's sake — never speculative.
- rul-one-drawn-channel-and-its-proof `[TYPED danger-sense]` — a drawn gloss
  WILL be read as the fact and transitively carries the fact's full burden:
  spatial relations (order, causation, co-temporality) in output are
  correctness-critical the moment they exist. Exactly ONE drawn channel
  carries semantics; every other edge-family travels as ref-text (robust
  under wrapping). The drawn channel is redundant-by-law (every rail-event
  is also a stated record field) and owes a NEW correctness story, covered
  by none of the existing approaches: a generative/analytical checker — a
  dumb graph-PARSER of separate dev-lineage from the generator, literally
  reading columns/rows and comparing semantically against the model — never
  committed goldens. Long-term; tabled.
- lean-shadow-rails-default — the one expensive drawn channel goes to what
  the user demonstrably CANNOT infer from their own text, weighted by
  SPARSITY: wall/mutation shadows (permeable vs total) beat fact-reach
  (dense, medium-comprehensible via refs), derivation (mostly local),
  dataflow (visible in the user's own text), and claim-consumption
  (cross-file; undrawable on a book spine). Gutter width then honestly
  reads as "how much epistemic danger is active across this line" — visual
  complexity living where epistemic complexity lies (hard-acked as a goal;
  hard in practice). Fact-rails remain an optional rail family. When the
  restricted us-hosted multi-host dialect lands, its limited cross-host
  synchronization becomes the dominant gutter inhabitant where present.
- tune-survival-is-half-a-feature `[TYPED]` — survival is NOT universally
  valuable: to the solo/homelab half it is the draw and the wow-factor; to
  any organizational user it is effectively DOA (no survival will ever be
  possible past an `ansible-playbook` or `terraform apply` line — large-zoo
  ops universes do not admit local shell-analysis). The solo half is
  earlier, larger, and flakier (serves-me-the-first-time matters most
  there), so defaults favor it — but surfaces support both, and org-reality
  delegation walls appear in honest renders.
- lean-code-shaped-presentation-skeleton — the render leans CODE-SHAPED:
  the regenerated sh CFG as the spine (the audience's thoroughly-understood
  map; the plan render's sibling), with records as comment-stanzas
  subordinate to sites and terse fate-comments trailing code lines. The
  surface is explicitly NOT book-byte-honest `[TYPED]`: the book bytes are
  a presentation skeleton through which value is threaded — valueless/
  no-causation lines droppable, inert argv skimmable, all omissions typed
  in the exclusion ledger (numbered gaps confess them, the plan render's
  own idiom). Loops: silently constant-prop and write the body once per
  member when sufficiently local (the admin knows they didn't write
  `provision-node beta` — repetition is understood); several styles owed
  with a heuristic cutover for complex CFGs `[TYPED]`.
- rul-refs-are-short-urls `[TYPED]` — opaque refs get ONE entrypoint
  (`dorc r <any-id>`), identity-type packed byte-brutally into the id for
  registry-less routing, and are VERSION-UNSTABLE by open contract (same
  inputs + same Dorc version reproduce them; nothing survives versions —
  stability lives in expectations, modes, and results, not bytes). Every
  ref printed anywhere must fully expand into a complete, standalone,
  user-runnable command it abbreviates. This also satisfies the
  stateless-narrowing gate: the expansion IS the reproducible re-entry.

## §3 the model (the reduced state-space; lean-ack tier)

Sorting rule applied: for each candidate axis — is it a per-datum FIELD, a
STRUCTURE edge-family (navigated, never projected-along), a DERIVED view
(computed at projection, never stored), or the WRAPPER (above the plane)?
Thirteen flat axes reduced to:

**Per-datum fields (granularly assigned at the leaves `[TYPED]` — hoisting
to containers creates laundering/drop pressure; the influence account's
carried-never-stamped law is the precedent):**

1. **speaker** — speech-act kind × speaker-set; committee-capable, with
   inseparable-committee a first-class state of the set; terminal element
   "me". From: `SpeechAct` (typed, deliberately unordered), source
   identities, engine mint-sites. New piece: the committee-set.
2. **world-coordinate** — one moment × host × attempt-lineage per datum,
   never a document frame. From: receipt species, correlation identities,
   per-step apply ordering, explicit now-observations. Consequence: the
   claimed/predicted/at-apply/now "quadruple" is not stored shape — it is
   four co-subject data compared at render; the recorded/re-derived
   agreement matrix is a derived comparison. (Deliberate drop: authorship
   moments are untracked; source drift is digest-compare, not timestamps.)
3. **subject** — typed refs into structure: recorded site, cell coordinate,
   region, value slot, decision. From: durable locators + the tidied report
   API (`30Rk`'s three items are this field's constructors). NB the standing
   enrichment debt: 'site' itself is historical and owed a more granular
   replacement (human note, 2026-08-30; ~`plans/30L`-adjacent; held on the
   frozen kernel — do not build against 'site' as if permanent).
4. **payload** — kind-tagged content: measurement · prediction-set
   (MAY/MUST-graded possibility sets — the plan-time lattice, distinct from
   apply-reported concrete values, which are separate measurements; local
   per-statement collapse at render is keyed by the license kind) · decision
   (the mechanism vocabulary) · license · value (with `ValueGrade`) ·
   negative-space assertion (decline / wall / can't-tell / absent-from-this-
   carrier — all AFFIRMATIVELY KNOWN facts about not-knowing, each with its
   own remedy) · narrative. LEAST-SETTLED of the five (the human followed
   1/2/3/5 explicitly; this one is really a type-family, not an axis).
5. **source-of-delivery** — which carrier delivered it: a receipt/record BY
   REF (auth/completeness looked up on the carrier entity, never copied), or
   LIVE — the same reporting machinery is drivable in real time from live
   data (`dorc apply --why` is an owed shape) without reingest, exactly as
   reingest runs without live rederivation. The model must not bake
   ingest-only assumptions `[TYPED]`.

**The wrapper (above the plane, per-slot) `[TYPED]`:**
`Knowable(T) | KnowableNYI | Unknowable(reason)` — an enriched Option,
consumed by construction. `Unknowable` = by-construction-FOREVER class;
reason is compile-time, for reviewers, discarded at runtime. `KnowableNYI` =
not-yet-piped, argument-free, census-gated so it cannot silently ship as the
product's voice. Everything else — including every failed-to-know-but-TRIED
state — lives inside `Knowable(T)` as plural, domain-owned data: a probe's
can't-tell, an author's decline, a truncated record, and (the subtle cell)
KNOWN-ABSENT-FROM-THIS-CARRIER: a v1 receipt that never recorded a field is
neither NYI (piping cannot help immutable bytes) nor Unknowable (future
receipts can know it) — it is an affirmative fact about the carrier.
Laundering NYI upward into Unknowable is the named builder failure-mode this
wrapper exists to prevent. Fences: the wrapper binds the REPORT PLANE only
(the license plane keeps its welded bottoms); admission outcomes
(Admitted/NoObservation/Refused) are never paraphrasable into it.

**Structure (edge-families; must exist and be walkable):** derivation
operands (conclusion→inputs — the display for suspected engine-wrongness is
the conjunction of a conclusion with its operands, since the engine cannot
see its own illogic) · program topology (CFG/dataflow position, loop
membership, region→instance aggregation) · the receipt graph · the locus DAG
(with per-namespace coordinate projections + the match/`né` state).

**Derived at projection (never stored):** trust-ordering (= the existing
`Knowability` projection over speaker — not new); remedy-routing (speaker ×
subject × structure; forked where the speaker-set is inseparable);
question-relevance; the agreement matrix; encoder-withholding (an
EXIT-boundary event: the spine holds the value, the encoder gates the door;
renders as a typed withhold).

**Type-opportunity (flagged, not assumed):** speaker and payload-kind are
correlated but not derivable; making the ILLEGAL (payload-kind × speech-act)
pairs unrepresentable would promote `AID-NEEDS:law-trust-tier-is-syntax`
from rendering law to construction law — a claim in measurement's clothing
becomes uncompilable, the report-plane cousin of the `Must→May`-only
coercion.

**Open residuals:** how the committee speaker-set carries members' authoring
loci without duplicating structure into a field · wrapper granularity
(lean: one domain-level truncation fact over a half-delivered payload, not N
wrapped fields) · whether consented-speech carries scope or derives it from
the invocation record (lean: derives).

## §4 the flagship moment (doctrine, for the deferred surface)

The situation is epistemically inverted: the user holds the one bit the tool
lacks (the universe is wrong); the tool holds everything else (every place it
participated, declined, or took someone's word). The surface's product is
the join — a participation ledger the user's one bit propagates through.
Wrongness concentrates where trust was spent: naked at-most claims and
early-closed definitions, then inseparable committee speech, then vouches
(the adequacy gap), then in-window measurements, then the tool's own
derivations — last because invisibly wrong, presentable only as
operand-conjunctions. Completeness includes negative space; exoneration
("nothing here is mine — your wrongness is elsewhere") is a valid, valuable
outcome and only works if participation coverage is total. Every thread tip
is a cheap discriminating check in the world; every remedy names its locus
and reach; forked remedies stay forked. Within-class ranking by blast
surface (what each trusted item licensed) is a -GUESS, unadjudicated.

## §5 the r30 close-out direction (build now, deliberately temporary)

Build now — one lane — with this honesty stated up front: the first cut of
the surface is INTENTIONALLY TEMPORARY. The difficult presentation work —
the drawn gutter with its own correctness story, the settled register — is
deferred until it can get proper process and attention (§6, §7); what ships
now is the reconstruction and an unpretty total surface over it, expected
to be replaced without ceremony:

- **The reconstruction plane** — the §3 model as real types; pure function
  of injected inputs (receipts, typed current-source observations, synthetic
  worlds); deterministic by discipline and DST-covered by EXTENDING the DST
  boundary outward — explicitly NOT kernel-resident ("kernel" stays reserved
  for the apply-correctness-critical core; the receipt family's dependency
  constraints keep this plane outside it, and quarantined 30R invariants are
  respected where narrowly relevant); sealed report-plane, recorded-stays-
  recorded, encoder-mediated exits.
- **The total surface** — the no-decisions render: everything the
  reconstruction holds, exactly once, structurally, axes intact, zero
  selection. This is PRODUCT-FACE, not a debug artifact `[TYPED]`: for the
  "I have grep, give me my data" half of the userbase it is the second or
  third reach, and serving it well through the tool (reconstruction +
  multi-receipt collation upstream of THEIR filters) beats them jq-ing raw
  receipts. Register, for this cut: interim — canonical content-derived
  ordering, stable label vocabulary, no prosodic headers or summaries
  (broad statements over "everything we know" are near-vacuous by
  construction), and no drawn gutter (the graph library is §6-owed); the
  settled register is deferred (§7) and this cut is replaced without
  ceremony when it arrives. The fence still binds: readability affordances
  are structure and labels only — glossing re-enters deferred territory.
- **`--json` sibling** — the same reconstruction, mechanically serialized;
  withholds as explicit typed markers, never silently-absent keys; loudly
  version-unstable pre-user (the CI-mode additive-envelope posture is the
  eventual contract; promising it now would be plan-as-API).
- **Gates** — totality census (every datum reaches the render exactly once;
  every constructible payload kind reaches output); NYI census;
  determinism/byte-stability across seeds and timing permutations; DST
  spreading over adversarial and weird receipt-sets (wrong vouches, fallen
  claims, TOCTOU-shaped histories, damaged carriers); discovery floors.
- **The durable-gap audit falls out** — every axis v1 receipts cannot
  populate surfaces as typed carrier-absence in the total output; whether
  the NEXT durable version records it is then a reviewed widening
  (`rul-durable-contents-reviewed-before-design`), decided per-hole with
  evidence in hand, pre-user while widening is cheap.

## §7 render-register explorations (STRAWMEN ONLY — read-optional)

> Everything in this section is stylistic state-space exploration,
> non-critical to reading this note. Raw examples: `30Vc`.

Four generations were drawn and adjudicated in one sitting:

1. **gen-1** (`30Vb`): prosodic first-person renders over the USER_STORY
   book. Superseded wholesale — register, grounding, and addressing all
   re-ruled afterward.
2. **gen-2**: single-line greppable `key=value` records, sh-parseable.
   VERDICT: dead as the default (cannot encode the axes readably in one
   line; `--json` owns premeditated filtering) — but it found the correct
   LEVEL of non-prosody, and survives as the `--oneline` option.
3. **gen-3** (`30Vc` §2): multi-line block-records, moment-minor, one
   drawn shadow-gutter, three sites fully enumerated over a dense
   13-line book exercising nearly every axis (coverage table included).
   VERDICT: good tracking of WHAT-to-communicate-at-which-intersection
   (density, ordering, layout); surface itself rejected — too prosodic,
   and Unicode is a nope (ASCII only, ever).
4. **gen-4** (`30Vc` §3): full structural verbosity (gutter + regenerated
   CFG spine + comment-stanza records) with factoids at a terse
   debugging register (`meas svc:wg-mesh@active=y p07:02:13`). VERDICT:
   mostly turned out WORSE — over-corrected past the readable middle.

Standing conclusion: the correct register sits BETWEEN gen-2's prosody
level and gen-3's density/ordering, ASCII-only, code-shaped per §2's
skeleton lean — and the human's meta-finding stands: AI-generated strawmen
work for source code (which is prosodic) and break down for data-dense UI
design, so the register question resolves against real generated output
under a human eye, not against further strawmen. `27V:rul-output-form-
unwelded` is thereby confirmed empirically, twice.
