## §1 — The verdict vocabulary

- **transcript-editable** — the words render as a stamped editable section in an owning
  case; the flow is `$EDITOR` the `.loom` → `mise run loom:compile` → `loom:promote`;
  every case's `edit-loop:` line teaches it in place.
- **lock-tier-by-mechanism** — the words live in a generated lock and are edited by the
  sanctioned hand-seed (serializer field order; the byte-identity fixpoint proves the
  seed), because a named transport mechanism does not reach them; each such row carries
  the remedy's name and price.
- **never-loom, law-cited** — a standing law makes a transcript face structurally
  impossible; the law is named per row.
- **blocked-on-X** — a firing world or render seat does not exist yet; the blocker is
  named and owned elsewhere (these are NOT deferrals of loom work — the loom machinery
  is complete for them the day X exists).
