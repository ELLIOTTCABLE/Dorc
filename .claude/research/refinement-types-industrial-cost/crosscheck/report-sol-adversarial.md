# Findings

## High severity

- consumer-safety-assumes-comparison-safety — +SURE. The Lean spike does not prove that truthful generators compose into safe verdicts. `SoundCompare` directly assumes that every `same` result denotes one cell and every `provablyDisjoint` result denotes non-overlapping cells; the consumer theorems then merely project those assumptions. No theorem derives `SoundCompare` from generator-specific truth conditions, so the advertised conclusion—misfires can arise from a lying generator but never from the composition algebra—is not established.

  Evidence: `.claude/.../spike-lean-sparing/REPORT.md`, `modeling-note-conditional-soundness`, lines 143–154; `SparingAlgebra/Laws.lean`, §3, lines 168–198 and `consumer_map_safety_inversion`, lines 268–282. The report itself flags `SoundCompare` as statement-uncertain at lines 30–33, but then describes a stronger result than the theorem states.

- public-minters-defeat-unrepresentability — +SURE. Two of `claim.rs`’s four advertised compile-time absence properties are not unrepresentable. An observation payload can be explicitly reminted with `Claim::vouched(measured.into_observation(), Rung::Both)`, and `ByVouch<P>` can expose any `P` through `vouch`/`into_vouch`; no bound prevents `P` from being a fact-plane type.

  Evidence: `.claude/research/refinement-types-industrial-cost/turn03-2026-08-13-notes.md`, lines 34–40, claims four properties are enforced by absence-of-code and compile-fail tripwires. `spike/crates/core/src/claim.rs`, `TC-tier-1` and `TC-tier-3`, lines 27–48; the tripwires test only automatic `.into()` and a nonexistent `.observation()`, lines 94–110. The public payload exits and unrestricted vouch minter are at lines 238–278 and 280–300. The nominal tier separation still prevents accidentally passing `ByObservation` where `ByVouch` is required; what fails is the stronger provenance-laundering and fact-plane-absence claim.

- formal-model-omits-relation-generators — +SURE. The spike formalizes an entity/selector subset, not `notes/277` §2 “as written” or the current law registry. Axis-invariance, lend/re-keying, contradiction handling, and the cross-context transport/carry mechanisms are absent; context inequality simply yields `unknown`.

  Evidence: `.claude/.../spike-lean-sparing/REPORT.md`, lines 3–6, claims coverage of `notes/277` §2. `Research/notes/277-entity-algebra-design.md`, §2 generator registry, lines 133–144, includes the omitted generators. `SparingAlgebra/Compare.lean`, comparison definition and preceding scope note, lines 83–101, explicitly excludes invariance and pure-predicate carry; `SparingAlgebra/Sparing.lean`, lines 64–66, says cross-context transport is not modeled. `spike/CLAUDE.md`, `context-entry-probing` and `pure-predicate-carry`, lines 245–272, shows that these are current composition laws, not incidental detail. The omission is disclosed locally in code, but it is absent from the report’s seven spec gaps and conflicts with its headline coverage claim.

## Medium severity

- prior-plan-authorization-is-misread — +SURE. The synthesis calls the solve certifier a pre-authorized execution of earlier plans, but those plans authorize something narrower and later: property tests or possibly a Why3 lemma for the inert-classification/elision kernels, explicitly deferred to v2 and “do not adopt up front.” They do not authorize building a generic post-fixpoint certifier now.

  Evidence: `.claude/research/refinement-types-industrial-cost/turn06-2026-08-13-notes.md`, portfolio item 1, lines 12–19. `Research/plans/021-static-analysis-engine.md`, §1, lines 12–20; `Research/plans/055-analysis-architecture.md`, decision 6, lines 93–99. The certifier may still be a sound recommendation, but “pre-authorized” and “kVERIFY weld not reopened” do not follow from the cited plans.

- failed-edge-is-not-causal-localization — +SURE. A failed post-fixpoint edge witnesses that the partial state violates one constraint; it does not necessarily localize an oscillation or identify its cause. At cap-trip, arbitrary downstream states may simply be stale, and the first reported edge depends on traversal order; the underlying violation might instead be non-monotonic transfer, unbounded height, non-semantic equality, or an earlier cycle.

  Evidence: `.claude/research/refinement-types-industrial-cost/turn06-2026-08-13-notes.md`, lines 24–27, claims the first failing edge localizes the oscillation and provides a pointable cause. `spike/crates/analysis/src/solve.rs`, preconditions and cap semantics, lines 57–69, and worklist update/cap behavior, lines 90–121. `Refused(EdgeWitness)` remains useful narrative evidence for why certification failed, but it must not be attributed as the root cause.

- generic-top-fallback-is-unavailable — +SURE. The proposed certifier can be generic over the existing `Lattice` trait, but its advertised `degrade-to-⊤` result cannot: `Lattice` has no `top`, and important domains deliberately cannot implement `BoundedLattice`. The amendments’ `Certified | Refused` shape can repair this by putting the stage-0 fallback outside the lattice, but that outer mapping is not specified in the combined record.

  Evidence: `.claude/research/refinement-types-industrial-cost/turn06-2026-08-13-notes.md`, lines 12–17 and 20–25. `spike/crates/analysis/src/lattice.rs`, `Lattice` and `BoundedLattice`, lines 30–66; `MapL`, the stated workhorse domain, lines 178–185. The record should distinguish “generic checker returning refusal” from “consumer-specific control flow choosing a conservative floor.”

The individual costs are recorded; the joint agent-loop, upgrade, and proof-repair cost is not priced.

  Evidence: `.claude/research/refinement-types-industrial-cost/turn06-2026-08-13-notes.md`, Flux portfolio item, lines 44–58; the ack ledger, lines 102–106, says Flux is only a typed lean, not an adoption decision. `crosscheck/input-turn06-pending-amendments.md`, Aeneas architecture and sequencing, lines 62–94, targets the facade, checker, and reference algebra while saying the architecture remains pending acknowledgement. This is an unresolved success-case decision, not evidence that the two tools cannot be complementary.

# Considered and rejected

- source-grading-did-not-launder-load-bearing-claims — +SURE. I did not find a load-bearing literature claim whose recorded grade erased a known limitation. The record calls the SymCrypt material promotional and cost-free, marks proof-stability evidence `~SUSPECT`, records the three-way Aeneas pin and manual loop-invariant burden, and quarantines the initial summarizer-derived material rather than citing it. Evidence: `turn01-2026-08-13-notes.md`, `METHOD DEFECT` and `Unverified first-pass material`, lines 25 and 340–459; `turn05-2026-08-13-notes.md`, `maturity, churn, proof stability`, lines 55–68 and `SymCrypt pilot state`, lines 96–106; `sources.json`, SymCrypt grading, lines 238–240. The prior-plan authorization finding above is a citation misread, but not grading laundering.

- amendment-supersession-is-not-a-contradiction — +SURE. The late-trigger model decision and the survival-field-trial premise are explicitly killed and replaced, with the conductor’s original contradiction acknowledged. Treating the amendment as authoritative yields a coherent new timing rule: cheap Rust checks now, formal work at spec settle, followed by the later Aeneas spike-gated lean. Evidence: `crosscheck/input-turn06-pending-amendments.md`, `amendment-survival-reprioritized`, lines 7–58, and `amendment-aeneas-pulled-forward`, lines 62–94. The remaining Flux/Aeneas disposition gap is narrower than an internally contradictory plan.

- individual-tool-costs-are-not-sanitized — +SURE. The broad suspicion that pin, sync, and agent-loop costs were hidden does not survive. Flux’s nightly/source-build/binary/ICE/SMT costs are explicit; Aeneas’s rustc–Charon–Lean pin, weekly churn, manual invariants, and proof-repair sessions are explicit; the Lean spike reports manual bootstrap, deprecated tooling, and roughly 1 GB of toolchain state. Evidence: `turn06-2026-08-13-notes.md`, lines 44–58 and 190–204; `turn05-2026-08-13-notes.md`, lines 55–68; amendments, lines 89–94; Lean `REPORT.md`, `toolchain experience`, lines 183–231. The costs are not quantified well enough for a final adoption decision, but they are honestly surfaced.

- zero-sorry-status-is-technically-honest — +SURE. The machine-checking claim itself is not misleading: the report states the exact axiom profile and repeatedly warns that Lean checks proofs rather than statement faithfulness. The defect is the strength and scope of the statements, especially `SoundCompare`, not the reported checker result. Evidence: Lean `REPORT.md`, lines 8–12, statement-uncertainty lines 30–33, and LLM-viability discussion lines 233–265; amendments, lines 96–125, also disclose that the adversarial review and adjudication never ran.

- declared-spec-gaps-should-not-be-recharged — +SURE. Compare directionality, top self-sameness, top-member granularity, empty footprint, missing disjoint generator, redundant minting conditions, and the missing `same` payload are openly identified. I did not count those as new breaks merely because they are serious. Evidence: Lean `REPORT.md`, `SPEC-GAPS`, lines 35–141.

- certifier-is-not-foreign-ground-truth — +SURE. The record explicitly says the certifier shares the transfer model and catches solver/result bugs rather than model or authored-claim bugs. That limitation is not concealed and should not be charged again. Evidence: `turn06-2026-08-13-notes.md`, ack ledger, lines 139–144.

- must-order-duality-matches-the-code — +SURE. The claim that `Must<L>` permits one solver to cover may/must order orientations is borne out: it swaps bottom/top and join/meet under a `BoundedLattice` constraint. Evidence: `spike/crates/analysis/src/lattice.rs`, `Must<L>`, lines 267–301. This does not supply the missing generic fallback top discussed above.

+SURE None of these findings invalidates a decision marked as human-typed. The survival lane’s priority, pre-network failure preference, narrative obligations, two-plane firewall, and willingness to explore Flux/Aeneas remain coherent strategic rulings. The findings instead invalidate conductor-level claims that the Lean artifact has discharged composition correctness, that claim-tier laundering is mechanically unspellable, that the certifier was already authorized by the cited plans, and that an edge witness identifies an oscillation’s cause. The Flux and Aeneas entries are themselves recorded as leans or acknowledgement-pending shapes, so resolving their overlap requires a new human decision rather than reversal of an existing typed one.
