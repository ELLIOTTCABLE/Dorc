## Findings

- P1 has a named, proved academic ancestor: **scope graphs** — a scope is DEFINED as a
  minimal program region behaving uniformly w.r.t. name resolution, with three checkable
  uniformity properties; 28Q's "frame" is that object and can borrow the definition and
  the well-formedness obligations wholesale. [A-neron-scope-graphs-2015]
- The same theory says sh's `.` is an **include, not an import** — and include is a
  DIFFERENT visibility policy (drop the local-shadows-imported rule), which their
  efficient environment algorithm explicitly does not support. P2's "sourcing IS the
  package boundary" is spelling an include as a module; that costs the fast algorithm.
  [A-neron-scope-graphs-2015]
- Their WF(p) ∈ P*·I* restriction exists to forbid "import a module, then walk to ITS
  lexical parent" — behaviour they say no real language exhibits. sh's `.` exhibits it by
  construction (the sourced text runs in the caller's environment and sees the caller's
  ambient definitions). So the load plane's crossing rule cannot be inherited from the
  module literature unexamined. [A-neron-scope-graphs-2015]
- Cyclic/mutually-recursive imports are the one corner their calculus admits ANOMALIES in
  even with a seen-set: one derivation can resolve a single import two different ways.
  Their read: no real language has mutual imports, "perhaps for good reason". A direct
  argument for 28Q refusing cyclic `.`-closures rather than deduping them.
  [A-neron-scope-graphs-2015]
- **Optimistic analyses are wrong when halted early; pessimistic ones are merely poor.**
  Anything in the kernel that starts from "assume live/assume converged and lower on
  evidence" cannot be budget-capped, timed out, or read off mid-fixpoint. This prices
  every "analysis budget" idea against the plan's fail-on-human-timescales posture.
  [A-wegman-zadeck-sccp-1991]
- Reachability/executability flags must key to **edges, not nodes** — node-keying provably
  loses facts (their Fig. 13 loop). The kernel's positional keys should be transitions
  between statements, not statements. [A-wegman-zadeck-sccp-1991]
- A def-use-chain-shaped index is both quadratic (N² chains per variable) AND less precise
  than an SSA-shaped one, because def-use chains exist along non-executable paths. 28Q §1's
  "every derived row keyed by DefinitionId + a frame→live-definition map" is def-use-chain
  shaped; the φ-function/renaming discipline is the known repair, and 28Q's built
  hash-munge of plural bodies is already SSA renaming in disguise.
  [A-wegman-zadeck-sccp-1991]
- Positive prior-art for the unification thesis itself: fusing constant-propagation with
  unreachable-code elimination into ONE fixpoint "gets better results than are possible by
  repeated applications of the separate algorithms" — the same claim 28Q §1 makes when it
  retires the agreement veto, `live_source`, and `never_live` as three separate mechanisms.
  [A-wegman-zadeck-sccp-1991]
