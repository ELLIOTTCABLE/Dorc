## Findings

Vacuity and the empty set (the single most-reported failure family)
- Novices' faulty formulas are more often too permissive than too strict: 13.95% underconstrained vs 8.81% overconstrained, with 23.62% both, across 96k Alloy4Fun submissions [A-jovanovic-novices-write-alloy-models-2024].
- `x in S` and `x.f = y.g` are true when the left side (or both sides) is empty; `some x & S` is the non-vacuous form. An Alloy board member hit this in 'a couple of models I'm working on', and a learner hit it twice; Jackson calls the empty-set treatment deliberate, and an Alloy 6 author says a lint would drown in warnings [B-alloy-discourse-in-pitfall-empty-lone-2023] [A-jovanovic-novices-write-alloy-models-2024].
- `Door.state = Unlocked` is not `all d: Door | d.state = Unlocked` when Door is empty; `in` is the matching relational form [B-alloy-discourse-time-in-electrum-style-2020].
- 'No counterexample found' observed from an implication whose premise was unsatisfiable for the quantified case [B-alloy-discourse-no-counterexample-vacuous-implication-2022].
- `check name {}` checks the empty constraint, not the assertion of the same name [A-practicalalloy-commands-in-detail-2025]. Likewise `run done {...}` treats a predicate's name as a label and never applies it; a practitioner lost about a week to this, and it is confirmed undocumented [B-alloy-discourse-run-label-shadows-pred-2020].
- Enabling Prevent-overflows makes an obviously false check pass (10 is unrepresentable at the default bitwidth of 4); `1-1` is the empty set, with no warning [A-practicalalloy-working-with-integers-2025].
- An unexpectedly small unsat core signals a problem with the property or the bounds [A-danas-user-studies-model-finder-output-2017].

Facts that silently narrow the universe
- A fact makes the world's illegal states unrepresentable, so error handling cannot be checked. Practitioners and authors converge on preds activated per run/check, with facts kept for environment assumptions, uninteresting-case pruning, symmetry breaking, and cross-field definitions [B-wayne-alloy-facts-fiction-2024] [B-alloy-discourse-facts-vs-assertions-2020] [B-alloy-discourse-one-fact-or-many-2024].
- Observed live: a user made a failing temporal check pass by adding the fact `eventually some devices` [B-alloy-discourse-checking-a-function-2023].
- Inductive-invariant checking requires init and transitions to be predicates, not facts; fairness kept as a fact slows every safety check [A-practicalalloy-inductive-invariants-2025] [A-practicalalloy-safety-liveness-fairness-2025].
- Jackson (designer): every environment assumption is a risk, so constrain as little as possible and separate mechanism from environment [A-jackson-alloy-cacm-2019].

Alloy 6 temporal pitfalls
- A formula outside any temporal operator constrains only the first state. Observed as: a safety predicate that lacked `always` and checked only the initial state [B-alloy-discourse-db-migration-model-2023]; a non-temporal fact read as initial-state-only [A-practicalalloy-behavioral-modeling-2025]; learners reading such formulas as implicitly always [A-nelson-forge-teaching-formal-methods-2024]; and one of the two most common novice LTL fixes, inserting `always` [A-jovanovic-novices-write-alloy-models-2024].
- Unframed mutable relations change arbitrarily, with no warning (TLA+'s TLC errors instead). Practitioners call this 'criminally error prone'; the authors concede the fragility as the price of declarativeness [B-wayne-alloy6-about-time-2021] [B-alloy-discourse-frame-conditions-fragile-2023] [B-alloy-discourse-frame-condition-boilerplate-2022].
- Precedence and sequencing traps: `always` binds tighter than `or`, so an unparenthesised trace fact constrained only its first disjunct [A-alloy-discourse-state-machine-style-electrum-2020]; `init; always {...}` leaves the first transition unconstrained [B-alloy-discourse-init-semicolon-first-transition-2022]; `until` also asserts its right side eventually holds [B-wayne-alloy6-about-time-2021].
- `one` in a trace fact is discouraged by two Alloy 6 authors: it over-constrains and hides missing frame conditions; `some` plus frames is the house style [A-alloy-discourse-state-machine-style-electrum-2020] [B-alloy-discourse-init-semicolon-first-transition-2022].
- A stutter/skip event is recommended so deadlocked behaviours still extend to infinite traces; without it, valid traces go missing [A-practicalalloy-behavioral-modeling-2025] [A-alloy-discourse-state-machine-style-electrum-2020].
- The Alloy-5 time-signature idiom persists: in 2024, half of 50 analysed exam answers built an ad hoc `sig Time`, and half omitted `var` [B-padalino-alloy6-temporal-teaching-module-2024]; a 2026 machine-translated-looking forum model uses `util/ordering[State]` [D-alloy-discourse-chronorealm-review-request-2026].
- LTL cannot state properties comparing two traces (hyper-properties) [B-alloy-discourse-checking-a-function-2023].

Scope
- Default overall scope is 3 and bounds only top-level sigs; singleton subsigs eat it; one unscoped extension silently gets zero; `util/ordering` makes scopes exact and can inflate the parent's; Int bitwidth is separate from the overall scope [A-practicalalloy-commands-in-detail-2025] [C-alloy-discourse-ordering-scope-cascade-2026] [A-practicalalloy-working-with-integers-2025].
- Counterexamples needed larger bounds than defaults in practice: 5 time steps in the Hotel model [B-macedo-alloy-meets-tlaplus-2016]; ~20-event Chord traces against a searchable length under 5 [A-zave-lightweight-modeling-chord-2012]; 35-step AWS bugs found with TLA+ [A-newcombe-formal-methods-at-aws-2014]; a mutant only exposed at scope 5 after coverage saturated at 4 [B-andoni-evaluating-small-scope-hypothesis-2002].
- Events modelled as sigs can drop traces when the event scope is too small; the authors advise predicates plus reification in Alloy 6 [B-alloy-discourse-facts-vs-assertions-2020] [B-alloy-discourse-frame-condition-boilerplate-2022].

Regression and test idioms practitioners use
- `expect 1/0` exists for regression scripts (counting was abandoned as symmetry-dependent); Execute All is used after every change [B-alloy-discourse-expect-meaning-2022]; since 6.1.0 `expect 1` also turns symmetry breaking off [A-nelson-forge-teaching-formal-methods-2024] [A-practicalalloy-encoding-test-instances-2025].
- Test instances use the some/disj pattern pinning every sig and field, not singleton sigs (which leak into every command). Counterexamples are exported as predicates to bank as negative regression runs [A-practicalalloy-encoding-test-instances-2025]. Trace scenarios use the lowest-precedence `;` operator [A-practicalalloy-encoding-trace-scenarios-2025].
- To check a simplification, assert old <=> new [C-alloy-discourse-check-predicates-equivalent-2020]. To tell under- from over-specification in an equivalence check, an extra ShouldBeRejected/ShouldBeAccepted atom is used [A-macedo-teaching-alloy-alloy4fun-2020].

Growing and debugging models
- The designer's practice: grow one constraint at a time with runs before checks, run the bare data model first, and treat a bogus counterexample as a missing constraint; simulation finds the unknown unknowns [A-jackson-alloy-cacm-2019].
- Even experienced users struggled to build a model from a natural-language spec; they saw a wrong instance of `add` and did not recognise it [A-mansoor-empirical-study-alloy-modeling-2023]. Minimal counterexamples led students to fix self-loops only [A-danas-user-studies-model-finder-output-2017]. A user trusted 'never seen an instance' over a check that found a counterexample at once [A-alloy-discourse-state-machine-style-electrum-2020].
- Hardest constructs measured: closure over a relational expression (8% success), ordering module (16%), ternary relations (19%), nested temporal operators (32%) [A-macedo-teaching-alloy-alloy4fun-2020].
- Syntax traps: `Room lone -> Key` binds `lone` to `in`; `no x, y: A` is not nested `no`; reversed joins are silently valid [A-nelson-forge-teaching-formal-methods-2024]; not/no and &&/& confusion [A-macedo-teaching-alloy-alloy4fun-2020]; the type checker only catches always-empty expressions, so `extends` buys warnings that `in`-plus-facts does not [A-practicalalloy-type-system-2025].
- Reverse-engineering an inductive invariant oscillates between strengthening and weakening without converging [A-zave-lightweight-modeling-chord-2012]; preservation counterexamples may be unreachable states [A-practicalalloy-inductive-invariants-2025].
- Scaling a multi-module industrial model: `univ`-typed fields kill performance, CapacityExceeded appears at domain^arity > MAX_INT (var adds a column), composition slows solving, so split into smaller deterministic models [B-alloy-discourse-performance-industrial-model-2022]; one-sig static configurations took 12 minutes to build [C-alloy-discourse-bulk-static-data-2022].

Language models writing Alloy
- 2022: ChatGPT confused non-symmetric with anti-symmetric and treated fields as OO fields [B-alloy-discourse-openai-supports-alloy-2022]. 2024: GPT-4 models are 'not very clever or good' but correct well given a counterexample [C-alloy-discourse-alloy-and-llms-2024].
- In a feedback-loop repair study, failures were mostly counterexamples, then syntax errors, then repeating the input; progress stalled after 3-4 iterations; GPT-3.5 hallucinated constructs [A-alhanahnah-llm-repair-alloy-specs-2025].
- Field report (2026): agents write all Alloy in one company's specs; no measurable code-generation gain, but measured spec-defect finding, especially as specs evolve [C-alloy-discourse-agent-development-field-report-2026]. Skill authors report tuning toward pitfalls and house style rather than general Alloy, and no with/without-skill evaluation yet [C-alloy-discourse-ai-skill-for-alloy-2026].
- Forge authors note learners stumble on keywords sourced from generative AI tools that do not do what they wanted [A-nelson-forge-teaching-formal-methods-2024].

Counter-thesis
- AWS evaluated Alloy (pre-Alloy-6) after Zave's Chord work and chose TLA+ for expressiveness over rich data such as dynamic sequences of nested records [A-newcombe-formal-methods-at-aws-2014]. Alloy's bounded analysis limits temporal verification, and trace length had to be checked separately per bound in Alloy 4 [B-macedo-alloy-meets-tlaplus-2016]. An Alloy 6 author says Alloy is not suited to implementing interpreters or operational semantics [C-alloy-discourse-unbounded-scope-2023]. Zave judges graph properties easy in Alloy and awkward in Promela/Spin, and temporal properties the reverse [A-zave-lightweight-modeling-chord-2012]; Jackson reports she used the two in tandem [A-jackson-alloy-cacm-2019].
