# Findings: where Alloy and assay fought the author

> STRAWMAN, non-normative. The running record of every place the assay shape (`notes/30Y`) or Alloy 6 fought the author of this strawman, kept as it happened. Each entry carries a first-cut class: **mechanical** (Alloy or the runner needs tuning: a keyword, a scope, a parse rule), **shape** (assay's own conventions need tuning: a fence rule, a generated form), or **design** (the model as written did not say what Dorc's prose meant, and an answer was invented for this strawman only). The final division into "good correctness-chafe" and "tune the tooling" is written at the end, once the build has run. Grades: `+SURE` / `~SUSPECT` / `-GUESS`.

## Before anything ran (authoring the spec by hand)

- `fight-before-is-a-keyword` (mechanical, +SURE): Alloy 6 reserves `before` as a past-time temporal operator, so the harness field `Line.before` of strawman-2 cannot parse. Renamed `above` here and in the harness this strawman carries. assay's harness must avoid every Alloy 6 keyword, including the temporal set (`after`, `before`, `always`, `eventually`, `historically`, `once`, `releases`, `since`, `triggered`, `until`, `var`, `steps`).
- `fight-seq-length-needs-bitwidth` (mechanical, ~SUSPECT until run): a `seq` field's maximum length may not exceed the largest non-negative integer the bitwidth admits, and the default `seq` scope is the default scope, so `for 12 but 4 Int` should be refused. The shared `bookScope` here carries `6 seq`. assay must size `seq` when it sizes `Int`.
- `fight-query-atoms-need-scope` (shape, +SURE): strawman-2 made the sparing question a sig (`Query`) with a fact forcing one atom per writer-reader pair; a seven-line book has up to twenty-one pairs, and a scope that caps `Query` below that is a spurious unsat. The question is a predicate over two lines here, and no scope sizes it.
- `fight-field-names-clash-on-reverse-join` (mechanical, ~SUSPECT): two sigs sharing a field name (`Verdict.of` and a second `of`) make a reverse join such as `of.l` ambiguous. Every field name here is unique where a reverse join is written.
- `fight-writers-include-guarded-lines` (design, invented here): strawman-2's shared module asks the sparing question only of lines that are not converged. A guarded line is converged and may still run, since its guard falls through to the original bytes when the world drifted; if it runs it writes, so a converged line below it that reads what it writes must not elide. Here the lines that may run are every line not elided, guarded ones included, and the definition of `Elided` is a fixpoint over the lines above it, well-founded because `above` is a strict order. `~SUSPECT` this is what the design intends (`23O`: "an elided command casts no wall", whose contrapositive is that a non-elided one does); the prose never says it.
- `fight-at-most-claims-intersect` (design, invented here): a footprint is an at-most claim, so two true footprints for one verb bound the same tool from above and the sound reading is their intersection, not their union. With a union, adding a claim can widen a footprint and turn DISJOINT into UNKNOWN, and the monotonicity law fails on a free universe. Intersection it is; the corpus checks add that one describer speaks per verb so the case never arises in a book.
- `fight-line-indexed-truth-has-no-fence-form` (shape, +SURE): what a line actually wrote is world truth about that line, and `30Y` § 2.1 says a world fact never mentions `this` while a formula that does is the line's outcome. The truth stratum needs a third thing: a per-line world record. Encoded here as a world-object declaration on the line (`one sig … extends Touched {} { line = this  wrote = … }`), which the fence rule already admits, so no new introducer; but assay must know that a declaration on a line binds `this` whether or not it is a claim.
- `fight-unrecorded-truth-defaults-against-you` (design, invented here): a line with no `Touched` record is taken to have written every key, so a book that asserts a survival was not an under-execution must record what each wall really wrote. The alternative default (wrote nothing) makes every such assertion vacuous.
- `fight-one-literal-two-things` (design, deferred to 311): the literal `nginx` names a package on one line and a unit on another; a positional footprint would need the identity tier to split the word into keys. Footprints here name cells by introduced word, and the one positional claim (`cp`'s destination) names a literal no other line uses. `fact { all disj a, b: Key | a.w != b.w }` records that this document does not split words.
- `fight-redirects-are-argv-words` (shape, +SURE): assay v0 lexes the map line and takes everything after the first word as `argv`, so `>>/var/log/hork.log` and `2>&1` become argument words. Harmless here because no claim reads those positions; wrong the moment a footprint claim counts positions past a redirect.
- `fight-attribution-by-single-removal-is-masked` (design, invented here; the previous strawman's soft spot, sharpened): `restsOn` removes one claim at a time, so a false footprint that leaves a key out and a false separation of that key from the backing each license the same collision alone, and removing either leaves the answer standing; neither is named. Redundant speech is not the cause, two independent falsehoods are, and a one-voice premise does not exclude it. Attribution is therefore defined structurally here, as the speech the derivation used (`support`: the walls' footprints, the line's backing, the separations consumed), which is honest and sufficient by construction and is what 311 § 3.5's sentences describe. The counterfactual `restsOn` is kept beside it and its laws are expected red, so the counterexample is on record.
- `fight-adversary-forces-key-existence` (good chafe, +SURE): a book that expects a line to guard because the identity tier is silent about one key must also say that key exists (`some w.local_nginx_conf`), or the adversary drops the key, empties the backing of it, separates what remains, and elides the line. The pin is the assay design working: what the solver would otherwise choose, the book must state.
- `fight-speech-is-typed-claim-not-mdecl` (mechanical, ~SUSPECT): the harness types `speech` as `set Claim` while every spec function takes `set MDecl`; whether Alloy accepts the wider set at the narrower parameter is not known to the author, so a one-line `said[l]` narrows it everywhere. If Alloy accepts it unasked, the helper is noise; if not, assay's harness should type `speech` over the shared module's claim base.
- `fight-set-argument-to-scalar-parameter` (mechanical, ~SUSPECT): whether `by[Line]` (a set handed to a parameter declared `l: Line`) is legal is not known to the author; the book run spells six calls instead.
- `fight-empty-backing-spares-everything` (design, invented here): a `Reads` claim that names no positions and no cells says the verdict reads nothing, and a verdict that reads nothing survives any wall vacuously. That is true of a constant check and false of a claim that mis-counted positions; a fact requires that claimed positions exist on every matched line, and the survival law is stated only for non-empty backings.
- `fight-trivial-laws` (shape): three of strawman-2's laws restate their definitions (`attributionMinimal` is the definition of `restsOn`; "elided needs a vouch" and "withheld never elides" are the definition of `Elided`). Dropped; a check that cannot fail is the emilia specimen.

## From the strawman-2 run (the builder's lane, `notes/30Yb` § 5; observed, not authored)

- `fight-before-is-a-keyword` confirmed +SURE: Alloy's parser refused the field at the token.
- `fight-seq-length-needs-bitwidth` corrected: `for 12 but 4 Int` is accepted and *silently* clamps `seq` to 7; only an explicit `8 seq` is refused. assay must spell the `seq` bound it relies on, since silence there is a hidden cap, not an error.
- `fight-field-names-clash-on-reverse-join` widened: the clash also bites a *forward* join over a union (`all d: A + B | d.on` when both `A` and `B` declare `on`). Every quantifier over a union of sigs sharing a field name must be split. Mechanical, and a lint assay could carry.
- `fight-open-resolves-under-the-root-only` (mechanical, +SURE): every `open X` resolves to `X.als` under one root, the root file's directory minus the root's own `module a/b` path; opened modules never resolve relative to themselves; only the root module's commands run. So 30Y's `books/<name>.als` layout works only with a `module books/<name>` header, and a tree-global `shared` must be served into every document's root or the runner must overlay it (the builder's `--open`). Strawman-3 flattens instead.
- `fight-a-global-jdk-wins-over-the-pin` (mechanical, runner): a machine-global JDK earlier on `PATH` beat the mise pin; the task now names the pinned JDK by path.
- `fight-book-commands-blow-up` (mechanical by class, cause ~SUSPECT): every line check with a verdict and a query timed out at 300 s; one translation was two million variables and seven million clauses. The ceiling of twelve for every unowned kind, under `separated`'s per-pair transitive closures, is the suspect. Strawman-3 has no closures beyond `^above`; its numbers will say whether the ceiling or the closures are the cost.
- `fight-corpus-check-has-no-universe` (shape, +SURE): a corpus check that reads ownership through truth-in-force, run with `exactly 0 Line`, reads nothing as true and fails. assay's corpus universe must put every claim atom in force (one virtual line with all claims as speech, or corpus checks quantified without `True`).
- `fight-premises-unsat-at-the-default-claim-scope` (shape, +SURE, and the vacuity twins earning their keep): three of strawman-2's laws passed with no witness because `Claim` inherited the default scope of six and a non-vacuous `DISJOINT` needs seven claims. assay must size `Claim` on every law command, or the spec must state it; strawman-3's laws now carry `8 Claim`.
- `fight-laws-red-once-the-universe-fits` (design, the 311 translation's inheritance): at `8 Claim`, strawman-2's `monotoneInSpeech`, `strangerSafe`, `attributionHonest`, and `attributionSufficient` all have counterexamples (`neverWrongWhenAllTrue` holds). Not chased here; the counterexample for `strangerSafe` involves an unnamed speaker's `MayWrite` claim, which is a first lead: an at-most footprint from a stranger *narrowing* a writeset is a grant, not a withhold, and monotonicity over a union of footprints fails exactly as `fight-at-most-claims-intersect` predicts.
- `fight-trust-on-first-use-jar` (tooling, honest): the Alloy release publishes no digest; the pin is trust-on-first-use, and the Maven Central artifact of the same coordinates differs from the GitHub release asset.

## From the strawman-3 run (the builder's lane, `notes/30Yb` § 7; observed; the conductor's rulings beside each)

Seventy-six of eighty-one commands matched their expectation on the first run, every command under twelve seconds at the shared ceiling of twelve, which puts strawman-2's blow-ups on its transitive closures and not on the ceiling. The five reds:

- `fight-monotonicity-law-reads-backwards` (design; the adversary's best catch so far, +SURE): `weaker` is spelled with the stronger answer on the right (`UNKNOWN->DISJOINT`), so `a.*weaker` is `a` and everything stronger, and `answer[S] in answer[S2].*weaker` demanded that the *smaller* speech set already answer DISJOINT whenever the larger does: the law of a system in which speech can never help. Copied from strawman-2 into strawman-3 unread, and green in both only while the premises were vacuous. `monotoneInSpeech` and `strangerSafe` now read `answer[S2] in answer[S].*weaker`. A lesson for the 30Y plan: a law whose premise run was unsat had never been read by anyone, machine or author.
- `fight-a-key-separate-from-itself` (design, invented here): nothing forbade a `Separate` claim with both ends one key; one such claim, false and in force, licensed a survival past an undescribed wall, because a universe of one key made the everything-footprint and the backing the same singleton and the pair of it with itself "separated". Separation is a statement about two keys: `all s: Separate | s.a != s.b`. With that, an undescribed wall's footprint always contains the backing's own keys and no true or false speech can separate a key from itself, so `survivalRestsOnFootprints` holds without a truth premise.
- `fight-a-named-cell-is-a-key` (design, invented here; the second instance of `fight-adversary-forces-key-existence`): the two flagged books never said the `ufw_rules` key exists, so the adversary dropped it, emptied ufw's backing, and elided the line past hork. Rather than pin `some w.ufw_rules` in every book, the species now says a cell a describer names is a key by that naming (`all d: Reads, c: d.cells | some k: Key | k.w = c`, and the same for `Disturbs`); argument words stay the identity tier's to mint, which is why book seven still pins `some w.local_nginx_conf`. The laws' `Key` scope rose to six so every named cell in a six-word universe can exist.
- `fight-forward-join-over-a-union` (mechanical, +SURE, restating the strawman-2 entry): `all d: Disturbs + Reads + Withholds | d.verb` is refused as ambiguous although every member declares `verb` identically. The corpus check is three quantifiers now, in the spec and the build alike. assay could carry this as a lint or generate the split.
- `fight-bare-claim-atoms-exist` (shape, +SURE): Alloy accepts a `set Claim` argument at a `set MDecl` parameter and a whole set at a scalar parameter, so the two hedges in the earlier entries were unnecessary as syntax; but the adversary does mint bare `Claim` atoms outside `MDecl` (the harness's `Claim` is not abstract), and `said[l]` is what keeps them out of the speech the functions reason over. Either the harness makes `Claim` abstract with the shared claim base as its only child, or every spec narrows as this one does.
- `fight-report-shape-drifts-between-hands` (shape, minor): the hand-written report used `expected` and one row per book; the runner writes `result` and one row per command, with Alloy's own reading of the scope clause. The runner's shape is the one to keep, and the lock of `30Y` § 2.7 should be generated from it, never hand-written.
