- vers2-syn-3 +SURE: turn01's vers1-fd-compare-is-kind-owned is CONFIRMED as the unanimous
  prior-art consensus, six independent ways: purl refuses uniform cross-type comparison
  [B-purl-faq-no-uniform-compare-2025]; VERS delegates the compare-relation to a per-ecosystem
  `type` [A-vers-range-specifier-2026]; OSV's range types (SEMVER/ECOSYSTEM/GIT) do the same
  [A-osv-schema-version-ranges-2026]; Ansible's `version_type` names the scheme per use
  [A-ansible-version-test-2026]; GNU coreutils disclaims any standard for `sort -V`
  [A-coreutils-version-sort-2026]; and the ecosystem-owned algorithms are deeply quirky
  (deb-version tilde/epoch rules [A-deb-version-evr-sorting-2025], rpmvercmp pathologies
  [B-rpmvercmp-fedora-quirks-2012]). Consequence: the founding `.version-compare` verb has a
  natural home as a per-KIND role in the kind-owner family. Sharpened residual: rpmvercmp
  shows delegated comparators inherit real pathologies, and NO source anywhere addresses a
  kind with no orderable version string at all — vers1-q-compare-home is a genuine prior-art
  void, not an oversight.

  passing systems, and CVE's Quality Working Group outright forbids mixing them (purls in CVE
  records may not carry versions; all version info in the `versions` field)

  grounding/version qualifier being a *distinct segment* rather than part of entity identity —
  worth relaying onward to the block-settle conductor as corroboration.
- vers2-syn-5 +SURE (the structural insight of the round): for a third-party describer,
  capability-PROBING dominates version-ENUMERATION wherever the fact is probeable — the
  version→behavior map is exactly what rots and what the described party lies about
  (autoconf doctrine [C-autoconf-doctrine-wikipedia-2026]; MDN's three failure modes
  [A-mdn-feature-detection-2026]; four decades of UA-string deception
  [B-ua-string-history-zakas-2010]) — and version-keying survives precisely for the
  UN-probeable residue (autoconf's own `case $host_os` escape hatch
  [A-autoconf-manual-system-type-1999]; cross-compilation, where probes cannot run). Mapped
  onto Dorc: ~SUSPECT the oracle-probe architecture is *already on the winning side* of this
  natural experiment (an `is_converged` probe is a capability/state test, not a version map);
  and Dorc's un-probeable residue is precisely the TRAVELING claim (a footprint's at-most
  cannot be cheaply probed at the elision site) — i.e. the sharp-knife tier. The human's
  silence-gate lattice (explicit-version-match required only for traveling licenses) thus
  lands the version-key exactly where the prior-art tradition puts it: on the un-probeable
  residue. Structural alignment, banked.
- vers2-syn-6 ~SUSPECT (a re-read of f2's counter-evidence): pip's all-or-nothing hash mode
  [A-pip-secure-installs-hash-checking-2024] initially reads as counter-evidence to the
  per-feature pin-gradient — but pip gates ONE trust-domain all-or-nothing (a partially-hashed
  file is a hole in a single boundary), which maps onto Dorc as *within-tier* coherence
  (partial pinning inside the traveling-license tier would be incoherent — the same logic as
  the touches() all-or-nothing footprint rule, TC-4), NOT as an argument against the
  cross-tier lattice itself. The lattice survives; the lesson is that each tier's gate must be
  all-or-nothing within its own trust boundary.
- vers2-syn-7 +SURE: the inline-pin position is STRENGTHENED — the forces that justify
  sidecar lockfiles (machine-regenerated transitive-resolution graphs, "not meant to be
  edited") are largely absent for Dorc's per-descriptor human-owned pins (f2-fd8;
  [A-filippo-gosum-not-a-lockfile-2026] [A-lockfile-design-space-arxiv-2025]) — with one leak
  to design for: a bare inline pin cannot say what-it-pins or where-to-look-for-updates;
  inline ecosystems grew a comment-annotation and a derived sidecar-cache to carry that
  (f2-fd3, [B-renovate-aur-jamie-magee-2023]). Dorc's pin-emission grammar must carry
  provenance/update-intent IN-BAND and sh-spelled, or the leak reopens as a comment-parse
  pressure against the kOOB redline. Model: Homebrew's opt-out spelled as code in the
  descriptor [A-homebrew-autobump-2025]. Two adoption/UX laws to copy: generate-by-default
  (the single biggest lockfile-adoption predictor [B-nesbitt-lockfile-tradeoffs-2026]) and
  bot-proposes-human-merges with a legible companion string beside the opaque pin
  [B-emmer-pin-github-actions-2025].
- vers2-syn-8 +SURE: two emission-tooling failure modes to design against, both from passing
  sources: (a) the silent-stale-pin — change a fetcher parameter without changing the hash and
  the old output is silently reused; emission tooling must force-invalidate on change, never
  treat hash-silence as consent [A-nixpkgs-fetchers-chapter-2025]; (b) hash-mismatch on an
  in-place upstream change is a HUMAN-adjudication trigger, never an auto-bump
  ("innocent respin ... or malicious") [A-gentoo-manifest-devmanual-2024] — the
  innocent-respin-vs-attack cell, mapping directly onto turn01's hostile-host/TOFU fence

- vers2-syn-9 +SURE: window-shape guidance for the comparison-keyed gate
  (vers1-position-comparison-keyed): the mid-narrow grammar candidate is OSV's
  sorted-signpost/events model (introduced/fixed/last_affected with 0/* sentinels), not an
  interval algebra [A-osv-schema-version-ranges-2026] [A-vers-range-specifier-2026]; epochs

- vers2-syn-10 +SURE: rot-governance prior-art is real and directly reusable: third-party
