## The never-do ledger (appended same-day at human direction — promoted from an in-chat teaching exercise)

The prior-art pass distilled to prohibitions, each grounded in the graded base. Marks:
**[HOLD]** = adhere fully; breaking it requires new evidence, not taste. **[BEND: …]** = Dorc's
odd position (third-party describers; fail-toward-run; the plan as a native consent surface)
grants unusual license, scoped in the mark. Certainty-marked where the claim is synthesis
rather than the corpus's own statement.

- never-universal-compare **[HOLD]** — never build, or let the engine quietly imply, a
  cross-ecosystem version comparison. The nearest-to-unanimous result in the pass: purl
  refuses it outright ("no reliable and uniform way")
  [B-purl-faq-no-uniform-compare-2025]; VERS delegates the compare-relation entirely to a
  per-ecosystem `type` [A-vers-range-specifier-2026]; OSV splits SEMVER/ECOSYSTEM/GIT because
  one order cannot serve [A-osv-schema-version-ranges-2026]; coreutils disclaims its own
  `sort -V` as non-standard [A-coreutils-version-sort-2026]; dpkg/rpm are mutually
  incompatible and internally quirky [A-deb-version-evr-sorting-2025]
  [B-rpmvercmp-fedora-quirks-2012]. Comparison is ecosystem knowledge ⇒ kind-owner CODE,
  never engine semantics. Inherited fine print: a delegated comparator imports its
  ecosystem's pathologies, and no surveyed system handles a kind with no orderable version
  at all — that cell is ours to invent (vers1-q-compare-home).
- never-version-inside-identity **[HOLD]** — never fold version/grounding into the entity
  coordinate. Identity names the referent; the window is a claim about it; the passing
  systems keep the axes separate and CVE's QWG forbids versioned purls outright
  [A-cve-qwg-expanding-software-ids-2025]. The relay-paragraph's
  qualifier-as-separate-segment, with external precedent.
- never-enumerate-the-probeable **[HOLD]** — never key behavior on a version→behavior map
  where the fact can be probed. Forty years of UA-sniffing rotted into every browser lying
  about its identity [B-ua-string-history-zakas-2010]; MDN's three failure modes (the string
  lies; capability drifts within a version label; enumeration excludes describers never met)
  [A-mdn-feature-detection-2026]; autoconf's doctrine is the same conclusion from ops
  [C-autoconf-doctrine-wikipedia-2026] — and even autoconf keeps a version/vendor escape
  hatch for genuinely un-probeable facts [A-autoconf-manual-system-type-1999]. ~SUSPECT the
  Dorc mapping: probes ARE capability-keys (already the winning side), and the un-probeable
  residue is precisely the TRAVELING claim — which is where the silence-gate lattice puts
  the explicit-version-match. The rule and the lattice agree without coordination.
- never-hash-as-authored-surface **[HOLD]** — never make content-hashes the thing users read
  and write. Unison proves the model works AND that it costs the whole surrounding
  text-tooling ecosystem (no stable FFI, no git-diffability, every tool rebuilt, pre-1.0
  after ~12 years) [A-unison-big-idea-2021] [B-unison-lwn-2024]. Fatal to the off-ramp
  redline. Intent stays the surface; hashes are internal/emitted form
  (vers1-position-surfaced-contract's compile-to-hash reading).
- never-registry-anchored-identity **[HOLD]** — never make identity depend on a
  centrally-curated dictionary. CPE: extrinsic identity + curation bottleneck ⇒ <50% CVE
  coverage since 2024-02 and industry flight to intrinsic purl
  [C-tomalrich-cpe-on-way-out-2025] [A-cve-qwg-expanding-software-ids-2025]. Independently
  validates the no-registry weld.
- never-auto-accept-pin-drift **[HOLD]** — on a pin mismatch at unchanged coordinates, never
  auto-bump: innocent-respin-vs-malicious is mechanically undecidable; Gentoo mandates the
  human diff old-vs-new and RECORD the verdict [A-gentoo-manifest-devmanual-2024]. Maps onto
  the hostile-host/TOFU fence; fail-toward-run makes the safe default cheap. Sibling rule,
  same tooling: never-trust-pin-silence — an unchanged hash is never consent when inputs
  changed (nixpkgs' silent-stale-pin: change the url, keep the hash, old output silently
  reused, everything green) [A-nixpkgs-fetchers-chapter-2025]; emission tooling
  force-invalidates.
- never-bare-pins **[HOLD — and HARDER for us than for prior art]** — never emit a pin that
  cannot say what it pins and where updates come from. Every inline-pin ecosystem sprang a
  leak here: the `# renovate:` tracking-comment; Arch's `.SRCINFO` derived sidecar
  [B-renovate-aur-jamie-magee-2023]. For prior art the leaks were shrugs; for Dorc they are
  a kOOB breach waiting (comment-parsing pressure returns through the back door). The clean
  model is Homebrew: machine-relevant intent spelled as code in the descriptor
  (`no_autobump!`, `livecheck … skip`) [A-homebrew-autobump-2025].
- never-silent-rewrite-of-authored-files **[BEND: mechanism, not principle]** — the settled
  norm for bots touching human files is propose-don't-mutate (PR + legible companion string
  beside the opaque pin) [B-emmer-pin-github-actions-2025], plus generate-by-default as the
  single biggest adoption predictor [B-nesbitt-lockfile-tradeoffs-2026]. Principle kept
  absolutely; the license: Dorc owns a STRONGER native consent surface than a PR — the plan —
  so emission can be plan-shaped rather than PR-shaped, and the legible-companion should be
  the sh expression itself rather than an annotation (~SUSPECT).
- never-partial-pins-inside-one-boundary **[BEND: the boundary placement is ours]** — pip
  forbids partially-hashed requirements as worse than none (the attacker routes through the
  unhashed line) [A-pip-secure-installs-hash-checking-2024]. Generalized: within one
  trust-domain a pin-gate is all-or-nothing or it is theater. The license is structural:
  Dorc has TIERS — the cross-tier gradient (self-tier gradual, traveling-tier gated) does
  not violate the rule, it relocates the boundary; within the sharp tier the rule bites in
  full (rhymes with the standing TC-4 all-or-nothing footprint rule).
- never-tight-windows-by-default **[BEND: we can afford tighter than most]** — pessimistic
  upper bounds are "often wrong in both directions" with transitive lock-in
  [B-rubygems-pessimistic-operator-2024]; OSV prefers `fixed` over `last_affected` because
  the latter rots into false negatives [A-osv-schema-version-ranges-2026]. Default windows
  open-ended; prefer boundary-events that do not age. The license: in package managers
  over-tightness breaks builds transitively; in Dorc it only demotes a site to guard/run — a
  value-cost, never a correctness-cost — and on the sharp-knife tier underclaiming is the
  point. Bound on the license: the hash-churn argument — tighten past the churn-rate and the
  feature stops firing at all.
- never-timeless-traveling-claims **[BEND: adopt the shape later, not the machinery]** —
  third-party claims measurably rot and good systems encode it: OSV stamps publication-time
  ceilings [A-osv-schema-version-ranges-2026]; TUF makes freshness a signed, EXPIRING,
  re-vouched property with scoped revocable delegation [A-tuf-freshness-expiry-2024]. The
  license: statelessness re-measures the world every run, so Dorc's one genuinely timeless
  traveler is the published oracle itself — the rule demands the oracle-contract's eventual
  freshness story (the MH2 tether, lockin-3 restated from the rot side), not TUF machinery
  now.
- never-assume-author-authority **[BEND: near-total — this IS our license, stated once]** —
  Bourgon's rule ("the version as expressed by the author is the core truth"; deriving
  identity from observed API strips authors of authority) [B-bourgon-siv-unsound-2020]
  mostly INVERTS for Dorc: our describers are not the authors, and author declarations are
  weak signal at scale (decade-long 0.x giants [C-zerover-version-means-nothing-2018];
  1-in-6 top crates violating their own semver [B-predrag-semver-rust-2024]; same
  version-string, different bytes across distros). Observational, mechanical, best-effort
  grounding is the only floor available to a third-party describer. Inherited UNBENT:
  mechanical compatibility is undecidable — derived grounding stays best-effort and is
  never sold as sound.
- never-do-meta ~SUSPECT (the one-sentence take-away): the prior-art world bifurcates on
  exactly the rot-asymmetry — author-is-versioner systems produced lessons that cap out at
  syntax, while the third-party-describer systems (distro packagers, vuln DBs)
  independently converged on the same four moves Dorc already made or is weighing:
  intrinsic identity, kind-owned comparison, identity/window separation,
  probe-over-enumerate. This ledger is mostly THEIR scar tissue; the unoccupied cell —
  surfaced per-callsite contracts authored by non-authors — remains genuinely ours, with no
  scar tissue to borrow (vers2-syn-2).
